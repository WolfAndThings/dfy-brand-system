Our Roofing Guarantee — Chattanooga Roofing Crew

A roof is not something you want to think about twice. When Chattanooga Roofing Crew installs or repairs your roof in Chattanooga, we guarantee the work is done right the first time. Here is exactly what that means.

The Chattanooga Roofing Crew Guarantee

Manufacturer-Spec Installation

We install every component — shingles, underlayment, flashing, vents, ridge caps — to manufacturer specifications. This is not just about quality; it is about your warranty. Manufacturer warranties are voided when installation deviates from spec. Our certified installers ensure your warranty is valid from day one.

Workmanship Warranty

Beyond the manufacturer material warranty, Chattanooga Roofing Crew provides a workmanship warranty on all labor. If a leak develops due to an installation error — improper flashing, missed nail pattern, inadequate sealant — we come back and fix it at no cost. Period.

Complete Cleanup

Roofing generates debris — old shingles, nails, felt paper, packaging. We run magnetic nail sweepers across your entire yard, driveway, and landscaping beds. We haul all old materials. Your property should look better when we leave than when we arrived, minus the old roof.

Property Protection

We tarp landscaping, cover outdoor furniture, protect windows and siding from debris, and lay plywood on driveways to prevent damage from falling materials. If our work damages any part of your property, we repair or replace it at our expense.

100% Satisfaction

Walk the property with us after the job. If anything does not meet your expectations — cleanup, appearance, flashing detail — we address it before we leave. If you notice an issue after, call within 30 days and we return at no charge.

What Is NOT Covered

  • Damage from subsequent storms, fallen trees, or acts of nature after installation
  • Pre-existing structural issues in the roof deck that were documented before work began
  • Cosmetic variations in shingle color between manufacturing lots (industry standard variance)

How to File a Concern

Call 423-555-0187 and ask for the project manager. Include photos if possible. We respond to all warranty concerns within 48 hours and schedule an inspection promptly. Our reputation is built on standing behind our work.
