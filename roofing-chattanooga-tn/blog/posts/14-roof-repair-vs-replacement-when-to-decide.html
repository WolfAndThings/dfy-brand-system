Roof Repair

Roof Repair costs $300-$2,000, same day in Chattanooga. Good if roof is under 15 years, extends life 5-10 years. This option works best for homeowners who want reliable results and are willing to invest in quality.

For most Chattanooga projects, roof repair is the right choice when you need professional-grade results that will last. Chattanooga Roofing Crew can help you determine if this is the best fit for your specific situation.

Full Replacement

Full Replacement runs $8,000-$25,000, 1-3 days in Chattanooga. Best if roof is 20+ years, new 25-50 year warranty. This is a solid option when budget is a priority or when the project scope is smaller.

Many Chattanooga homeowners start here and upgrade later if needed. The key is matching the solution to the problem — not every job requires the premium option.

Which Is Right for You?

  • Choose Roof Repair when you need professional results, warranties, and long-term value.
  • Choose Full Replacement when budget is the priority or the project is smaller in scope.
