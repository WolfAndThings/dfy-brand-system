Architectural vs 3-Tab Shingles for Chattanooga Homes

Architectural Shingles

Architectural Shingles costs $100-$150/square, 30-50 yr in Chattanooga. 130 mph wind rating, dimensional textured look. This option works best for homeowners who want reliable results and are willing to invest in quality.

For most Chattanooga projects, architectural shingles is the right choice when you need professional-grade results that will last. Chattanooga Roofing Crew can help you determine if this is the best fit for your specific situation.

3-Tab Shingles

3-Tab Shingles runs $70-$100/square, 15-25 yr in Chattanooga. 60-70 mph wind rating, flat appearance, budget option. This is a solid option when budget is a priority or when the project scope is smaller.

Many Chattanooga homeowners start here and upgrade later if needed. The key is matching the solution to the problem — not every job requires the premium option.

Which Is Right for You?

  • Choose Architectural Shingles when you need professional results, warranties, and long-term value.
  • Choose 3-Tab Shingles when budget is the priority or the project is smaller in scope.
