TPO vs EPDM Flat Roofing in Chattanooga

If you are comparing options for roofing in Chattanooga, TN, this guide breaks down the key differences so you can make the right decision for your project and budget.

Quick Comparison

FactorTPO MembraneEPDM Rubber
Cost$5-$8/sq ft, white reflective$4-$7/sq ft, black surface
Details20-30 year life, energy efficient, heat-welded seams25-30 year life, proven track record, glued seams

TPO Membrane

TPO Membrane costs $5-$8/sq ft, white reflective in Chattanooga. 20-30 year life, energy efficient, heat-welded seams. This option works best for homeowners who want reliable results and are willing to invest in quality.

For most Chattanooga projects, tpo membrane is the right choice when you need professional-grade results that will last. Chattanooga Roofing Crew can help you determine if this is the best fit for your specific situation.

EPDM Rubber

EPDM Rubber runs $4-$7/sq ft, black surface in Chattanooga. 25-30 year life, proven track record, glued seams. This is a solid option when budget is a priority or when the project scope is smaller.

Many Chattanooga homeowners start here and upgrade later if needed. The key is matching the solution to the problem — not every job requires the premium option.

Which Is Right for You?

  • Choose TPO Membrane when you need professional results, warranties, and long-term value.
  • Choose EPDM Rubber when budget is the priority or the project is smaller in scope.
