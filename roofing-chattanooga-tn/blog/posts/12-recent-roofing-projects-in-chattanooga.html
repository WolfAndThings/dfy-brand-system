Recent Roofing Projects in Chattanooga

Here is a look at recent roofing projects completed by Chattanooga Roofing Crew across the Chattanooga area. Every roof is different, and these examples show the range of work we handle for homeowners and businesses.

Full Roof Replacement — Signal Mountain

A 2,200 sq ft ranch home with 22-year-old 3-tab shingles that had significant granule loss and multiple soft spots in the decking. We completed a full tear-off, replaced 8 sheets of damaged OSB decking, installed synthetic underlayment with ice and water shield in the valleys and at the eaves, and re-roofed with GAF Timberline HDZ architectural shingles in Charcoal. Added a continuous ridge vent to replace the old box vents. Completed in 1.5 days.

Material: GAF Timberline HDZ | Time: 1.5 days | Included: Tear-off, decking repair, ridge vent upgrade

Hail Damage Insurance Claim — East Brainerd

After a spring hailstorm, the homeowner called us for a free inspection. We documented hail damage across the entire roof — bruised shingles, dented flashing, and damaged gutter faces. We worked directly with the insurance adjuster, were present during the inspection, and helped the homeowner through the supplemental claim process when the initial settlement was $2,400 short. Final result: full roof replacement covered by insurance with the homeowner paying only their deductible.

Material: Owens Corning Duration | Time: 2 days | Insurance: Full replacement covered

Metal Roof Installation — Lookout Valley

A homeowner upgrading from asphalt to standing seam metal for long-term durability and energy savings. The existing roof was only 15 years old but had already suffered two hail damage events. We tore off the asphalt, installed new synthetic underlayment, and installed a 26-gauge standing seam metal roof in Burnished Slate. The homeowner chose metal specifically for Chattanooga's hail exposure and the 50-year warranty.

Material: 26-gauge standing seam metal | Time: 3 days | Warranty: 50 years

Emergency Tarp and Repair — Red Bank

A large tree limb fell through the roof during a thunderstorm, creating a 6-foot hole through the shingles and decking into the bedroom below. We responded within 2 hours, tarped the opening with heavy-duty tarps secured with furring strips, and coordinated with the homeowner's insurance company. The permanent repair — new decking, underlayment, and matched shingles — was completed the following week once the insurance claim was approved.

Service: Emergency tarp + permanent repair | Response time: 2 hours | Insurance: Covered

Commercial Flat Roof — Hixson

A 4,000 sq ft commercial building with an aging EPDM membrane roof that had multiple patch repairs and persistent leaks around HVAC penetrations. We removed the old membrane, repaired the underlying insulation, and installed a new TPO membrane with fully welded seams and new flashing around all penetrations. Work was done over a weekend to minimize business disruption.

Material: TPO membrane | Time: 2 days (weekend) | Type: Commercial flat roof

Roof Repair and Ventilation Upgrade — Ooltewah

A 12-year-old roof in otherwise good condition was experiencing premature shingle aging on the south-facing slope. Inspection revealed inadequate attic ventilation — only 3 box vents on a roof that needed twice that airflow. We replaced damaged shingles on the south slope, removed the box vents, and installed a continuous ridge vent with proper soffit baffles. The homeowner also had us add a radiant barrier to the attic rafters to reduce summer heat gain.

Service: Repair + ventilation upgrade + radiant barrier | Time: 1 day
