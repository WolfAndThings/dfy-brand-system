What Affects Your Roof Replacement Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Roof square footage (measured in roofing squares — 1 square = 100 sq ft)
  • Number of layers to tear off
  • Roof pitch and complexity (valleys, dormers, hips)
  • Material chosen (architectural shingle, metal, tile)
  • Permit and disposal fees in Chattanooga

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Roof Replacement in Chattanooga

  • Get quotes from 3+ roofing contractors and compare scope line by line — tear-off, underlayment, ventilation, and cleanup should all be included
  • Schedule your replacement in late fall or winter when demand is lower and pricing is more competitive
  • Ask about manufacturer rebates and extended warranty programs — GAF and Owens Corning offer certified installer warranties
  • Check if your homeowners insurance covers any portion of the replacement, especially if the existing roof has documented storm damage
