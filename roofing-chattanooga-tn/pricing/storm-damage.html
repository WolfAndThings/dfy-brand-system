What Affects Your Storm Damage Repair Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Extent of damage (localized vs widespread)
  • Whether structural decking is compromised
  • Type of roofing material being repaired or replaced
  • Insurance claim coordination and documentation
  • Emergency tarping or temporary waterproofing needed

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Storm Damage in Chattanooga

  • File insurance claims promptly — most policies have a strict window for reporting storm damage
  • Get a detailed written scope of damage before repairs begin to support your insurance claim
  • Ask whether the roofer handles insurance claim coordination — many experienced storm damage companies do
  • Compare the insurance payout against 2-3 repair quotes to make sure the settlement is fair
