People Also Ask

Common questions about roofing in Chattanooga.

How much does a new roof cost in Chattanooga?+
A full roof replacement in Chattanooga costs $8,000 to $25,000 depending on size, materials, and pitch. Asphalt shingles are the most affordable at $8K-$15K. Metal roofing runs $15K-$25K but lasts 50+ years.
Does homeowner insurance cover roof replacement?+
If your Chattanooga roof was damaged by a covered event (hail, wind, fallen tree), insurance typically covers replacement minus your deductible. Chattanooga Roofing Crew helps with the claims process and works directly with adjusters.
How do I know if I need a new roof?+
Signs you need a new roof: shingles curling or missing, granules in gutters, daylight through roof boards, water stains on ceilings, or your roof is 20+ years old. Call 423-555-0187 for a free inspection.
How long does a roof replacement take?+
Most residential roof replacements in Chattanooga take 1-3 days depending on size and weather. We handle debris removal and cleanup as part of every job.
