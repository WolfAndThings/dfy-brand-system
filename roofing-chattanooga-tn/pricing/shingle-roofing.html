What Affects Your Shingle Roofing Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Total roof area in squares
  • Shingle grade (3-tab vs architectural vs designer)
  • Number of layers to remove
  • Complexity of roof layout (valleys, ridges, penetrations)
  • Ventilation and underlayment upgrades

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Shingle Roofing in Chattanooga

  • Choose architectural shingles over 3-tab for better wind resistance and curb appeal — the cost difference is often only 15-20%
  • Ask about impact-resistant shingles if your area gets hail — many insurance companies offer premium discounts
  • Verify the contractor is a certified installer for the shingle brand — manufacturer warranties require it
  • Bundle ventilation upgrades (ridge vent, soffit vents) with the re-roof for minimal added cost
