Emergency Roofing Service in Chattanooga, TN

Roof leaking? Storm damage? Do not wait. Chattanooga Roofing Crew provides 24/7 emergency roofing service across Chattanooga. We dispatch for active leaks, storm damage, and structural failures — call now and we respond within hours.

When to Call for Emergency Roofing Service

  • Active roof leak — water is coming through your ceiling during rain or snowmelt and you need it stopped immediately
  • Storm damage — high winds, hail, or fallen trees have visibly damaged your roof, removing shingles or puncturing the deck
  • Ice dam flooding — ice buildup at the eaves is forcing water under shingles and into your walls and ceilings
  • Structural sagging — visible sagging in the roof line, cracking sounds, or doors that suddenly will not close indicate potential structural failure
  • Blown-off shingles or flashing — sections of roofing material have been torn away, exposing the underlayment or deck to weather
  • Fire or impact damage — chimney fire, fallen tree limb, or other impact has compromised roof integrity

Response Time

For active leaks and storm damage, we aim to have a crew on-site within 2-4 hours during daylight. After-hours emergencies receive phone assessment immediately — we dispatch at first light for non-life-threatening situations. During major storm events, we triage by severity and prioritize structural failures and active interior flooding.

What to Expect

  1. Call with details — describe the damage, tell us if water is actively entering, and send photos if you can do so safely. Do not climb on a damaged roof. We assess urgency and provide immediate guidance.
  2. Emergency crew dispatch — we send a crew with tarps, emergency sealants, plywood, and all materials needed for temporary weatherproofing.
  3. Tarping and temporary seal — our crew covers the damaged area, seals active leak points, and stabilizes the roof to prevent further water entry until permanent repairs can be scheduled.
  4. Documentation and next steps — we photograph all damage for your insurance claim, provide a written report, and schedule permanent repair at the earliest available date.

After-Hours Availability

Our emergency line is monitored 24/7, including weekends and holidays. Roof emergencies during active storms are common — we respond as soon as conditions allow safe crew deployment. If water is actively entering your home, place buckets and move valuables while waiting. Call us first, then your insurance company.
