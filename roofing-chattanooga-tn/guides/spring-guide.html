Spring Roofing Guide for Chattanooga, TN

Spring is the most important season for roof inspections in Chattanooga. Winter storms leave hidden damage — cracked shingles, lifted flashing, and weakened seals — that will leak the moment heavy spring rain arrives. A spring inspection catches problems before they become expensive.

Your Spring Roofing Checklist

  • Post-winter roof inspection
  • Storm damage shingle repair
  • Flashing and seal assessment
  • Gutter cleaning and reattachment
  • Attic ventilation check
  • Moss and algae treatment

Post-Winter Inspection

Winter in Chattanooga punishes roofs. High winds lift shingle edges, ice dams force water under flashing, and freeze-thaw cycles crack seals that were tight in October. By spring, damage is hiding in places you cannot see from the ground. A professional spring inspection finds cracked shingles, popped nails, lifted flashing, and compromised boot seals before they leak into your attic and ceilings.

Storm Damage Assessment

If your roof survived winter storms without visible damage, it still needs inspection. Granule loss, micro-cracks in shingles, and shifted flashing are invisible from the driveway but will fail during the first heavy spring rain. Insurance claims for storm damage have time limits — get your inspection done early so documentation is ready if you need to file.

Moss and Algae

Shaded roof sections in Chattanooga develop moss and algae over winter. Left untreated, moss roots lift shingle edges and hold moisture against the roof deck, accelerating rot. Spring treatment kills growth and prevents it from spreading during the humid summer months.
