People Also Ask

Common questions about pressure washing in Greenville.

How much does pressure washing cost in Greenville?+
House washing in Greenville costs $200-$500, driveways $100-$300, and decks $150-$400. Pricing depends on square footage and surface type. Call 864-555-0131 for a free estimate.
Will pressure washing damage my siding?+
Not when done correctly. Greenville Pressure Pros uses soft wash techniques for vinyl and painted surfaces, reserving high pressure for concrete and brick. Wrong pressure settings can damage siding — that's why hiring a pro matters.
How often should I pressure wash my house?+
In Greenville's humid climate, we recommend house washing annually and driveway cleaning every 1-2 years. Regular cleaning prevents mold, mildew, and algae buildup that accelerates in SC humidity.
What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?+
Pressure washing uses high water pressure (2,000-4,000 PSI) for hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure with cleaning solutions for delicate surfaces like siding, roofs, and painted wood.
