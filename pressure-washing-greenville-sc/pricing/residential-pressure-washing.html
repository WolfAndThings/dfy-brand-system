What Affects Your Residential Pressure Washing Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Total square footage of surfaces being cleaned
  • Number of surface types (driveway, siding, deck)
  • Level of staining and organic growth
  • Access to water supply and electrical
  • Whether soft wash or high pressure is needed

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Residential Pressure Washing in Greenville

  • Bundle multiple surfaces (house + driveway + deck) into one visit for a package discount of 10–20%
  • Schedule in spring or fall when demand is moderate and crews have more availability
  • Ask about seasonal maintenance plans — annual or semi-annual service keeps surfaces cleaner and costs less per visit
  • Get quotes based on square footage, not hourly — hourly billing incentivizes slower work
