What Affects Your Concrete Cleaning Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Total square footage of concrete surfaces
  • Surface type (poured, stamped, pavers, exposed aggregate)
  • Level of embedded staining (oil, rust, paint)
  • Whether surface sealing is included after cleaning
  • Access for equipment and water drainage

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Concrete Cleaning in Greenville

  • Combine concrete cleaning with other surfaces being done the same day for a package rate
  • Ask about sealing after cleaning — sealed concrete stays cleaner longer and is easier to maintain
  • Power washing pavers dislodges joint sand — ask whether re-sanding is included in the quote
  • Schedule concrete cleaning in dry weather for best results and faster drying
