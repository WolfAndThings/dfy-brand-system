What Affects Your Fence Cleaning Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Linear feet of fencing
  • Material type (wood, vinyl, composite, metal, chain-link)
  • Height of fence
  • Level of staining and organic growth
  • Whether staining or sealing is included after cleaning

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Fence Cleaning in Greenville

  • Combine fence cleaning with staining or sealing in one appointment for labor savings
  • Clean both sides of the fence at once — one-sided cleaning looks worse than not cleaning at all
  • Ask about per-linear-foot pricing for fences over 100 feet for better transparency
  • Schedule fence cleaning before spring when mold and algae are easiest to remove
