What Affects Your House Washing Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Home square footage (exterior wall area)
  • Siding material (vinyl, wood, brick, stucco, fiber cement)
  • Number of stories
  • Level of mold, mildew, and dirt buildup
  • Whether soft wash or pressure wash is appropriate

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on House Washing in Greenville

  • Get house washing done before painting or staining exterior surfaces — it is required prep anyway
  • Schedule house washing in spring to remove winter buildup before pollen season compounds it
  • Ask whether the price includes window frame and sill cleaning — most good companies include it
  • Confirm the crew will soft wash if you have vinyl, stucco, or painted siding to prevent damage
