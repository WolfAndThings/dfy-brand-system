What Affects Your Gutter Cleaning Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Linear feet of gutters
  • Number of stories
  • Level of debris buildup (leaves, shingle grit, nests)
  • Whether exterior gutter face cleaning is included
  • Whether downspout flushing and testing is included

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Gutter Cleaning in Greenville

  • Bundle gutter cleaning with roof cleaning or house washing for a combined discount
  • Schedule gutter cleaning twice per year — late fall after leaves drop and late spring after pollen and seed pods
  • Ask whether the crew checks for and reports any gutter damage, sagging, or drainage issues
  • Consider gutter guards after cleaning — the upfront cost pays for itself in reduced cleaning frequency
