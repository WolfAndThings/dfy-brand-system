Our Pressure Washing Guarantee — Greenville Pressure Pros

We guarantee clean surfaces and zero damage. That is the deal. If a surface is not clean to your standard, we come back. If we damage anything, we fix it. Here is the full guarantee for every Greenville Pressure Pros pressure washing job in Greenville.

The Greenville Pressure Pros Guarantee

Surface-Appropriate Technique

We never use one setting for every surface. Concrete gets high pressure with surface cleaners. Vinyl siding gets soft wash. Wood gets adjusted pressure with fan tips. Roofing gets chemical treatment with zero pressure. We match the technique to the material every time — that is how we guarantee no damage.

Thorough, Even Results

No streaks, no missed spots, no zebra stripes. We overlap our passes, use consistent distance, and do a final inspection of every surface before moving on. If you see a spot we missed during the walkthrough, we hit it again immediately.

Property Protection

Before we turn on a machine, we cover plants, close windows, move outdoor furniture, and protect any items in the work area. Chemical overspray, water intrusion, and landscape damage are preventable — and we prevent them.

No Damage — Period

If our work damages any surface — stripped paint, cracked siding, broken seals, killed plants — we repair or replace it at our expense. Our insurance covers property damage, but our goal is to never file a claim because we do not cause damage in the first place.

100% Satisfaction

Walk the property with us when we finish. If any surface is not clean to your satisfaction, we re-clean it before we leave. If you find an issue after we are gone, call within 48 hours and we will return at no charge.

What Is NOT Covered

  • Permanent stains that we identified and disclosed before starting (some oil, rust, and paint stains cannot be fully removed)
  • Re-accumulation of dirt, pollen, or organic growth after cleaning (this is natural and not a defect in our work)
  • Pre-existing surface damage that cleaning reveals (peeling paint, rotten wood, degraded sealant often becomes visible only after grime is removed)

How to File a Concern

Call 864-555-0131 as soon as you notice an issue. Include photos if possible. We respond to all concerns within 24 hours and schedule a return visit if needed. Every concern is logged and reviewed to improve our process.
