Licensed & Insured Pressure Washing — Greenville Pressure Pros

Pressure washing involves high-pressure water, chemical solutions, and work on ladders and roofs. Damage to siding, windows, landscaping, and surfaces is possible with improper technique. Hiring a licensed, insured pressure washing company protects you. Here is what Greenville Pressure Pros carries.

Our Licenses & Credentials

  • State business license — registered and in good standing with the state of SC
  • City business license — licensed to operate in Greenville and surrounding municipalities
  • Environmental compliance — trained in wastewater management and runoff containment as required by local and state environmental regulations
  • PWNA certified — Power Washers of North America certification for proper technique, chemical handling, and surface-specific pressure settings
  • Soft wash certified — trained and certified in low-pressure chemical application for roofs, siding, and delicate surfaces

Insurance Coverage

  • General liability insurance — minimum \$1M per occurrence. Covers damage to your siding, windows, landscaping, vehicles, or any property affected during the job. Pressure washing can cause damage with incorrect pressure or technique — our insurance means you are never on the hook.
  • Workers compensation insurance — covers our crew for any injuries on your property, including falls from ladders or roofs during soft wash and gutter work.
  • Commercial auto insurance — covers our trucks and all mounted equipment.
  • Pollution liability — covers chemical runoff incidents, ensuring compliance with environmental standards and protecting your property.

Why This Matters to You

Unlicensed pressure washers are common — it is an easy business to start with a rented machine and a truck. But improper technique causes real damage: blown-out mortar joints, stripped paint, cracked vinyl siding, broken window seals, and dead landscaping from chemical runoff. When an uninsured operator damages your home, you pay for the repair yourself.

Greenville Pressure Pros carries full coverage because we take both our work and your property seriously. We match pressure and technique to every surface, and if something goes wrong, our insurance covers it.
