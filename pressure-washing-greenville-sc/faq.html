Pressure Washing FAQ

Last updated: March 2026

Common questions about our Pressure Washing services in Greenville, SC.

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?
Not when done correctly. We adjust pressure, nozzle distance, and technique for every surface type. Delicate materials like roof shingles and painted siding get our soft wash treatment — zero risk of damage.
Do you provide free estimates?
Yes. We provide free estimates in person or by phone. Most quotes take under 5 minutes. Just tell us what surfaces you want cleaned and we will give you a firm price.
What areas do you serve?
Greenville and all surrounding communities. Check our service areas page for the full coverage list.
How long does a typical job take?
A standard house wash takes 1-2 hours. Driveways and sidewalks take about an hour. Full-property packages (house, driveway, deck, walkways) usually take a half day. We will give you a time estimate with your quote.
What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?
Pressure washing uses high-pressure water to blast away dirt from hard surfaces like concrete and brick. Soft washing uses low pressure with cleaning solutions to safely treat delicate surfaces like roofs, siding, and painted wood.
Do I need to be home?
Not necessarily. If we can access water and the areas to be cleaned, we can work while you are away. We send before-and-after photos when the job is complete.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. We are fully licensed and insured. We carry liability coverage and workers comp. Proof of insurance is available on request.
