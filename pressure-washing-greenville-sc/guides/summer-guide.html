Summer Pressure Washing Guide for Greenville, SC

Summer is peak season for outdoor living — and outdoor cleaning. Algae and mold grow fastest in heat and humidity, BBQ areas need regular cleaning, and pool decks see the most traffic. Keeping surfaces clean in summer prevents buildup that becomes harder to remove by fall.

Your Summer Pressure Washing Checklist

  • Algae and mold prevention treatment
  • BBQ and outdoor kitchen cleaning
  • Pool deck and patio surface wash
  • Pre-party and event property cleaning
  • Commercial parking lot maintenance
  • Roof soft wash for summer algae

Algae and Mold Season

Heat and humidity are fuel for algae and mold growth. Shaded surfaces — north-facing siding, covered patios, under-deck areas, and fences against tree lines — are the worst offenders. Our soft wash treatment kills organic growth at the root, not just the surface. This means longer-lasting results compared to just blasting with water.

Outdoor Living Maintenance

If you are using your patio, pool deck, and outdoor kitchen all summer, those surfaces take a beating. Grease from the grill, sunscreen around the pool, food and drink spills on the patio — all of this builds up and stains if left untreated. A mid-summer cleaning keeps surfaces safe, clean, and looking good for the rest of the season.

Event and Party Prep

Hosting a summer party, graduation, or family gathering? A pressure wash the week before makes the entire property look maintained and inviting. Driveway, walkways, patio, siding, and fence — we can do a full-property refresh in a single visit.

Commercial Summer Maintenance

Storefronts, restaurant patios, parking lots, and commercial building exteriors all need more frequent cleaning in summer. Foot traffic is up, organic growth is at its peak, and customer impressions matter. Greenville Pressure Pros offers scheduled commercial maintenance throughout Greenville.
