Fall Pressure Washing Guide for Greenville, SC

Fall is about damage prevention. Leaf stains left on concrete become permanent. Gutters clogged with debris cause water damage. And sealing surfaces before winter requires clean surfaces first. Fall pressure washing protects your investment and prevents spring surprises.

Your Fall Pressure Washing Checklist

  • Leaf stain removal from concrete
  • Gutter cleaning and exterior wash
  • Pre-winter surface sealing prep
  • Commercial exterior fall refresh
  • Deck and patio end-of-season cleaning
  • Storm debris and dirt cleanup

Leaf Stain Prevention

Wet leaves left on concrete, brick, or stone will stain permanently within weeks. The tannins leach into porous surfaces and create dark outlines that no amount of scrubbing will fix. The solution is simple: remove leaves regularly and clean surfaces before stains set. If stains have already formed, our hot water treatment can often pull them out — but sooner is always better.

Gutter Season

Clogged gutters cause fascia rot, foundation water damage, and ice dams in winter. Fall is the critical time to clear them. We clean inside the gutters, flush downspouts, and wash the exterior gutter face so the whole system looks and works properly. One visit handles everything.

Pre-Sealing Surface Prep

If you plan to seal your driveway, deck, or patio before winter, the surface must be clean first. Sealant applied over dirt, mold, or algae traps it underneath and fails prematurely. Our cleaning ensures the sealant bonds properly and lasts through the winter.

Fall Commercial Refresh

Businesses heading into the holiday season want their exterior looking sharp. A fall wash before October cleans summer buildup and makes the property presentable for the busiest retail months. Call Greenville Pressure Pros at 864-555-0131 to schedule commercial fall cleaning.
