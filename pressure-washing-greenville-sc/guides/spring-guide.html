Spring Pressure Washing Guide for Greenville, SC

Spring is the single best time to pressure wash in Greenville. Winter leaves a layer of grime, mold, and organic buildup on every exterior surface. Pollen season is about to start, decks need opening, and curb appeal needs a reset. A thorough spring wash sets your property up for the entire year.

Your Spring Pressure Washing Checklist

  • House wash to remove winter grime
  • Driveway and sidewalk deep cleaning
  • Deck opening and prep for staining
  • Fence wash and refresh
  • Pollen season pre-treatment
  • Commercial storefront spring cleaning

Winter Grime Removal

By March, every exterior surface in Greenville has months of buildup — mud splash from rain, mold growth from moisture, leaf stains from fall, and general grime from winter weather. A full house wash, driveway cleaning, and deck wash removes all of it at once. Most homeowners are shocked at the color difference — they forgot what their siding actually looks like.

Deck Opening Season

Before you put the furniture out and start grilling, your deck needs attention. Winter leaves mold between boards, algae on the surface, and a slippery film that is both ugly and dangerous. We clean the entire deck surface, railings, and stairs, then it is ready for staining, sealing, or just using as-is.

Pollen Prep

In Greenville, pollen season coats everything in yellow-green dust. A spring wash before pollen hits means surfaces are clean and treated with solutions that slow re-accumulation. A spring wash after pollen means removing a thick layer of pollen plus everything underneath it. Either timing works — the key is doing it.

Spring Scheduling

Spring is our busiest season. Weekends in April and May fill up fast. Call Greenville Pressure Pros at 864-555-0131 early to lock in your preferred date. Weekday appointments are usually easier to schedule on shorter notice.
