Emergency & Same-Day Pressure Washing in Greenville, SC

Need surfaces cleaned today? Greenville Pressure Pros provides same-day and rush pressure washing for urgent situations across Greenville. When a deadline, inspection, or event is looming, we show up fast and deliver results.

When to Call for Emergency Pressure Washing

  • Pre-inspection cleaning — home inspection, city code inspection, or HOA compliance check scheduled within 24-48 hours
  • Event and party preparation — wedding, graduation, open house, or gathering at your property and surfaces look terrible
  • Insurance claim cleanup — storm, flood, or fire damage cleanup where surfaces need to be cleaned for adjuster documentation
  • Health code compliance — restaurant, commercial kitchen, or food service area needs immediate exterior cleaning to pass inspection
  • Real estate showing prep — home is going on the market or has a showing scheduled and curb appeal is suffering
  • Slip and fall hazard — algae, mold, or grease on walkways, decks, or commercial surfaces creating liability

Response Time

Same-day service is available for most jobs when you call before 10 AM. Rush service with a few hours notice is possible depending on crew availability. For commercial emergencies involving health code or safety compliance, we prioritize and can often mobilize within 2-3 hours.

What to Expect

  1. Call with your deadline — tell us what surfaces need cleaning, when you need it done by, and send photos if possible. We quote immediately and confirm availability.
  2. Rapid crew dispatch — we send a truck with the right equipment for your surfaces. Hot water, soft wash chemicals, surface cleaners — we come prepared to handle everything in one trip.
  3. Efficient, thorough cleaning — no shortcuts, but no wasted time either. Our crews know how to work quickly without sacrificing results.
  4. Walkthrough and confirmation — before we leave, we walk the property with you to confirm everything meets your expectations. Before-and-after photos provided on request.

After-Hours Availability

Our standard hours are 7 AM to 7 PM. For commercial clients with early morning or evening requirements, we accommodate special scheduling. Restaurant patios that need cleaning before a morning opening, commercial buildings that require off-hours work — we flex our schedule to match yours.
