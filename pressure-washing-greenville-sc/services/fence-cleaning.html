Will pressure washing damage my wood fence?

Greenville Pressure Pros uses low-pressure soft washing on wood fences in Greenville to prevent splintering and surface damage. We clean both sides when accessible and use cleaning solutions that kill algae and mildew without harming the wood.

How often should I have my fence cleaned?

Most fences in Greenville benefit from cleaning every 1 to 2 years. Fences near trees or in shaded areas may need cleaning annually to prevent algae and green buildup from taking hold.

Can you clean vinyl fences?

Yes. Greenville Pressure Pros cleans vinyl, wood, composite, and metal fences in Greenville. Vinyl fences respond especially well to soft washing and look brand new after cleaning. No scrubbing or harsh chemicals needed.

Do you stain or seal fences after cleaning?

Yes. Greenville Pressure Pros offers fence staining and sealing in Greenville after cleaning. Applying a stain or sealant after pressure washing protects the wood and extends the life of your fence by several years.
