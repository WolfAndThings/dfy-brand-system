5. Best Time of Year for Pressure Washing in Greenville

Timing matters — both for pricing and availability:

  • Spring — busiest season. Book early to avoid wait times.
  • Summer — high demand but longer daylight hours mean more scheduling flexibility.
  • Fall — great timing for pre-winter prep. Moderate demand, good availability.
  • Winter — lowest demand. Best availability and sometimes lower prices.

6. DIY vs. Hiring a Pro

Can you do it yourself? Sometimes. Should you? Here is how to decide:

FactorDIYProfessional
CostLower upfront (but hidden costs add up)All-inclusive pricing, no surprises
TimeTakes 3-10x longer without equipmentDone in hours, not days
SafetyRisk of injury without trainingTrained, insured crews
QualityVaries widelyConsistent, professional results
DisposalYou handle hauling and dump feesIncluded in the price

Bottom line: for small, simple tasks, DIY can save money. For anything involving heavy lifting, heights, chemicals, or significant scope — hiring a pro is safer, faster, and often cheaper when you factor in your time and disposal costs.

7. Red Flags to Watch For

Avoid companies that:

  • Cannot provide proof of insurance
  • Want cash only or refuse to give a written quote
  • Have no online reviews or a non-functional website
  • Pressure you to commit immediately
  • Significantly undercut every other quote (they will add fees later)
  • Cannot answer basic questions about their licensing or process
