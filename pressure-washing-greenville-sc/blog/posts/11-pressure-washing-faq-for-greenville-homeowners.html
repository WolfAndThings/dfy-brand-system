How often should I pressure wash my house in Greenville?

Every 1-2 years for most Greenville homes. Our humid climate promotes fast mildew and algae growth, especially on north-facing walls and shaded areas. Homes surrounded by trees or near water may need annual cleaning. Driveways and walkways benefit from annual cleaning due to heavy pollen and red clay staining.

What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water (2,000-4,000 PSI) to blast dirt off hard surfaces like concrete, brick, and stone. Soft washing uses low-pressure water (under 500 PSI) combined with cleaning solutions to safely clean delicate surfaces like vinyl siding, roofs, and painted wood.

Using the wrong method on the wrong surface causes damage. High pressure on vinyl siding can crack it, force water behind it, and void your warranty. Soft wash on a heavily stained driveway will not get the job done. A professional crew knows which method to use on each surface.

Will pressure washing damage my siding?

Not if done correctly. Vinyl and aluminum siding should always be soft washed, never hit with high pressure. Wood siding requires low pressure (under 1,500 PSI) with appropriate cleaning solutions. Brick and stone can handle more pressure but still need proper technique to avoid mortar damage. This is the main reason to hire a professional rather than renting a machine.

Is pressure washing safe for my landscaping?

Professional crews protect your landscaping by pre-soaking plants with water before applying any cleaning solutions and rinsing everything after the job. The diluted runoff at ground level is not strong enough to damage established plants. If a company does not mention plant protection during the estimate, that is a red flag.

Can I pressure wash my own house?

For flat concrete surfaces like driveways and walkways, a rental machine works if you are careful and use the right tip. For house siding, decks, and roofs, hire a professional. The risks of DIY on these surfaces include:

  • Cracked or warped siding from too much pressure
  • Water forced behind siding, causing mold and rot
  • Gouged or splintered wood on decks
  • Streaky, uneven results that look worse than before
  • Injury from high-pressure recoil or ladder work

What time of year is best for pressure washing in Greenville?

Any time temperatures are above 40 degrees. The most popular months are March through November. Spring cleaning removes winter grime and pollen. Fall cleaning preps surfaces before winter. Summer is fine but plan for faster drying of cleaning solutions in the heat.

How long does pressure washing take?

Typical timeframes for a professional crew:

  • House wash only — 1-2 hours
  • Driveway — 30-60 minutes
  • Full property (house + driveway + walkways + deck) — 3-5 hours
  • Roof soft wash — 1-3 hours

Do I need to be home during the service?

Not necessarily. We need access to an outdoor water spigot and the ability to reach all sides of the property. If those are accessible, you can leave a note with any special instructions and we will handle the rest. We take before and after photos so you can see the results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer recurring service plans?

Yes. Many Greenville homeowners set up annual or semi-annual cleaning schedules. Recurring customers get priority scheduling and typically save 10-15% compared to one-time pricing.

What if it rains on my scheduled cleaning day?

Light rain does not affect the cleaning — the surfaces are already wet. Heavy rain or thunderstorms will cause us to reschedule at no charge. We monitor the forecast and will call you the morning of your appointment if weather is a concern.

Are your cleaning solutions safe for pets?

We use biodegradable, EPA-compliant cleaning solutions. We recommend keeping pets inside during the service and for 30-60 minutes after until surfaces are rinsed and dry. Once dry, the treated surfaces are completely safe for pets and children.
