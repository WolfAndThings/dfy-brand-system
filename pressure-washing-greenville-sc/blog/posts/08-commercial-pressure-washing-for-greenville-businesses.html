Commercial Pressure Washing for Greenville Businesses

First impressions matter for any Greenville business. A dirty storefront, stained parking lot, or mildew-covered building exterior tells customers you do not care about details. Commercial pressure washing keeps your property clean, safe, and professional-looking year-round.

What Commercial Pressure Washing Covers

Commercial jobs are different from residential work. The surfaces are larger, the stains are tougher, and the scheduling has to work around business hours. Common commercial services include:

  • Building exteriors — brick, stucco, EIFS, metal panels, and vinyl siding
  • Storefronts and entryways — glass surrounds, awnings, signage areas
  • Parking lots and garages — oil stains, tire marks, gum removal
  • Sidewalks and walkways — gum, grease, and general grime
  • Dumpster pads — grease buildup and odor control
  • Drive-throughs — concrete lanes, menu boards, and pickup windows
  • Loading docks — oil, hydraulic fluid, and debris cleanup

Cost Breakdown for Commercial Pressure Washing in Greenville

Commercial pricing depends on the surface area, type of staining, and access requirements:

  • Storefront wash (single unit) — $150-$350
  • Full building exterior (small commercial, under 5,000 sq ft) — $400-$800
  • Full building exterior (large commercial, 5,000-20,000 sq ft) — $800-$2,500
  • Parking lot cleaning (per 1,000 sq ft) — $80-$150
  • Sidewalk and walkway cleaning — $0.15-$0.40 per sq ft
  • Dumpster pad cleaning — $75-$200 per pad
  • Gum removal (sidewalks) — $0.50-$1.00 per piece or included in surface cleaning

Many Greenville businesses save 15-20% by setting up a recurring service contract with quarterly or monthly visits.

Industries That Benefit Most

  • Restaurants and food service — grease buildup on sidewalks, drive-throughs, and dumpster areas creates slip hazards and health code issues
  • Retail stores — clean storefronts directly impact foot traffic and customer perception
  • Medical offices and clinics — cleanliness signals professionalism and patient trust
  • Property management companies — maintain curb appeal across multiple units or complexes
  • Gas stations and auto shops — oil and chemical stains on concrete require specialized treatment
  • Churches and community buildings — large footprints with high visibility

What to Expect: The Commercial Cleaning Process

  1. Site assessment — we walk the property with you to identify priority areas, stain types, and any surfaces needing special treatment
  2. Scheduling — work is scheduled during off-hours (early morning, evenings, or weekends) to avoid disrupting your business or customers
  3. Surface prep — degreasing agents are applied to oil stains, and pre-treatment solutions are applied to biological growth
  4. Cleaning — commercial-grade equipment (hot water, high GPM) is used for concrete and hard surfaces. Soft wash methods are used for building exteriors.
  5. Detail work — entryways, signage areas, and high-visibility zones get extra attention
  6. Wastewater management — we contain and manage runoff per Greenville's stormwater regulations
  7. Walk-through — final inspection with the property manager or business owner

Greenville Regulations for Commercial Properties

Greenville has stormwater management rules that apply to commercial pressure washing. Key things to know:

  • Wash water cannot flow directly into storm drains without containment
  • Chemical runoff (degreasers, detergents) must be captured or neutralized
  • Pressure washing companies should carry proof of compliance and proper containment equipment
  • Restaurant and food service properties may have additional health department requirements for exterior cleaning

A professional crew handles all of this. If a company does not mention stormwater containment during the estimate, that is a red flag.

Recurring Service vs One-Time Cleaning

Most Greenville businesses benefit from a recurring schedule:

  • Monthly — restaurants, gas stations, and high-traffic retail (dumpster pads, drive-throughs, entryways)
  • Quarterly — office buildings, medical facilities, churches (full building wash and sidewalks)
  • Semi-annually — warehouses, industrial buildings, property management portfolios
  • Annually — low-traffic commercial buildings, storage facilities

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work outside business hours?

Yes. Most commercial jobs are scheduled for early mornings, evenings, or weekends. We work around your operating hours so there is zero disruption to your customers or employees.

Do you handle multi-location accounts?

Yes. We service multiple locations across the Greenville area on a single contract. This simplifies billing and ensures consistent quality across all your properties.

Will pressure washing remove oil stains from my parking lot?

Most oil stains respond well to hot water pressure washing combined with commercial degreasing agents. Fresh stains come up almost completely. Old, deep-set stains may lighten significantly but may not disappear entirely. We will give you an honest assessment during the site visit.
