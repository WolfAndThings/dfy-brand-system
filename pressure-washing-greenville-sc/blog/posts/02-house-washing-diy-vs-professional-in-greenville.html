House Washing: DIY vs Professional in Greenville

Should you rent a pressure washer and do it yourself, or hire a professional in Greenville? Here is an honest breakdown to help you decide.

The True Cost of DIY Pressure Washing

  • Machine rental: $50-$100 per day
  • Cleaning chemicals: $20-$50
  • Water usage: $10-$20
  • Your time: 4-8 hours for an average house
  • Risk of damage: high-pressure water can damage vinyl siding, wood decks, window seals, and paint if used incorrectly

Total DIY cost: $80-$170 plus an entire day and risk of damage.

The Cost of Professional Service

  • Average house wash: $250-$400
  • Your time: 0 hours (you do not even need to be home)
  • Damage risk: professionals carry insurance and know the correct PSI for each surface

When DIY Works

  • Small flat surfaces like a concrete patio or single-car driveway
  • You already own a pressure washer and know how to use it
  • The surface is bare concrete with no nearby plants or painted surfaces

When to Hire a Pro

  • Your house — wrong PSI can push water behind siding, damage paint, or etch vinyl
  • Wood decks — too much pressure strips wood fibers and creates a fuzzy, damaged surface
  • Roofs — never pressure wash a roof; soft wash only or you void your shingle warranty
  • Second story — working with a pressure wand on a ladder is extremely dangerous
  • Mold and algae — proper chemical treatment kills the organism; pressure alone just moves it around

The Damage Problem

Every year in Greenville, homeowners cause thousands of dollars in damage with rental pressure washers:

  • Etched concrete from holding the wand too close
  • Stripped paint on wood trim
  • Water intrusion behind siding causing mold
  • Damaged window seals leading to fogged glass
  • Destroyed landscaping from chemical overspray
