Recent Pressure Washing Projects in Greenville

Here is a look at recent pressure washing projects completed by Greenville Pressure Pros across the Greenville, SC area. Every property is different, and these examples show the range of work we handle.

Full House Wash — Augusta Road Neighborhood

A two-story home with vinyl siding had not been washed in 4 years. The north-facing side was covered in green algae from the ground to the second-story roofline. Heavy pollen buildup on all sides made the white siding look gray. We soft washed all four sides, soffits, and gutter faces in under 3 hours. The homeowner said it looked like they had repainted.

Service: Whole house soft wash | Time: 2.5 hours | Surfaces: Vinyl siding, soffits, gutter faces

Driveway and Walkway Restoration — Taylors

A 50-foot concrete driveway with heavy oil stains from a leaking vehicle, plus red clay staining along both edges from years of splash-back. The front walkway and porch steps had black mildew growth in every crack and joint. We used hot water pressure washing with a surface cleaner for even results and applied a degreaser to the oil spots. The concrete went from dark gray back to its original light color.

Service: Driveway + walkway pressure wash | Time: 1.5 hours | Surfaces: Concrete driveway, walkway, porch steps

Pre-Sale Full Property Clean — Five Forks

A realtor called us to prep a listing. The home needed everything — house wash, driveway, back patio, fence, and a large wood deck. The deck had not been cleaned in years and was covered in gray weathering and mildew. We completed the entire property in one day. The realtor scheduled listing photos for two days later and said the photos generated significantly more showing requests than comparable listings.

Service: Full property package | Time: 5 hours | Surfaces: Vinyl siding, concrete, wood deck, vinyl fence

Commercial Storefront — Downtown Greenville

A restaurant on Main Street needed their sidewalk, patio area, and building facade cleaned before a weekend event. Gum removal on the sidewalk, grease staining on the patio concrete, and general grime on the brick facade. We completed the work early on a Wednesday morning before the restaurant opened. The owner set up a recurring monthly service after seeing the results.

Service: Commercial exterior wash + sidewalk + patio | Time: 3 hours | Surfaces: Brick facade, concrete sidewalk, stamped concrete patio

Deck Restoration — Simpsonville

A 400 sq ft pressure-treated pine deck with heavy mildew growth and gray weathering. The homeowner wanted to stain it but the old surface was too far gone to accept stain properly. We cleaned the deck with a wood-safe solution at low pressure, applied a brightener to restore the wood's natural color and open the grain, and the homeowner had a professional stainer finish the job three days later. The deck went from gray and splotchy to a rich, even finish.

Service: Deck wash + brightening | Time: 2 hours | Surfaces: Pressure-treated pine deck and railing

Roof Soft Wash — Mauldin

A 15-year-old architectural shingle roof with black algae streaks covering about 60% of the visible surface. The homeowner's insurance company had flagged it during a policy review. We soft washed the entire roof with our standard algae treatment. The black streaks began fading immediately, and the roof looked almost new within a week as rain washed away the remaining dead algae. The homeowner sent the before and after photos to their insurance agent and the issue was resolved.

Service: Roof soft wash | Time: 2 hours | Surfaces: Architectural shingle roof

What These Projects Have in Common

Every project above shares the same pattern: the property looked significantly older and more neglected than it actually was. Dirt, mildew, algae, and staining accumulate so gradually that homeowners stop noticing. A single day of professional cleaning takes years off a property's appearance.
