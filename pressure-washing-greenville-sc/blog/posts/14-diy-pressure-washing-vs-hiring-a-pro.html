DIY Pressure Washing vs Hiring a Pro in Greenville

Renting a pressure washer from a Greenville hardware store costs $50-$100 per day. Hiring a professional costs $200-$500 for most residential jobs. That price gap tempts many homeowners to go the DIY route — but the real cost comparison is more nuanced than the rental fee alone.

True Cost of DIY Pressure Washing

When you add up everything involved in a DIY pressure wash, the savings shrink fast:

  • Equipment rental: $50-$100/day for a consumer-grade unit (2,000-3,000 PSI)
  • Fuel or deposit: $15-$30 for gas-powered models
  • Cleaning solutions: $15-$40 for detergent and surface-specific chemicals
  • Nozzle tips and extensions: Often not included; $10-$25 extra
  • Your time: 4-8 hours for a typical house + driveway (vs 1-2 hours for a pro crew)
  • Pickup and return trip: 1-2 hours of driving and loading

Total realistic DIY cost: $100-$200 + a full day of labor.

Cost of Hiring a Professional

  • Driveway only: $100-$250
  • House wash (siding): $200-$450
  • House + driveway + walkways bundle: $350-$550
  • Full property (house, driveway, deck, fence): $500-$800

Pros and Cons of DIY

Pros:

  • Lower out-of-pocket cost if your time has no dollar value
  • You can work on your own schedule
  • Good for small, simple jobs like a patio or walkway

Cons:

  • Consumer-grade equipment is weaker than professional machines (2,500 PSI vs 4,000+ PSI)
  • Wrong pressure or technique etches concrete, strips paint, and damages wood
  • No insurance — if you crack a window or damage siding, you pay for it
  • Rental units lack surface cleaners that eliminate streaking on driveways
  • No access to soft wash chemicals for siding and roofs

Pros and Cons of Hiring a Pro

Pros:

  • Commercial-grade equipment delivers better results in less time
  • Proper technique for each surface type — no guessing
  • Insured against property damage
  • Soft washing available for delicate surfaces
  • Job done in 1-3 hours vs your full day

Cons:

  • Higher upfront cost
  • Need to schedule and coordinate
  • Must vet the company (insurance, reviews, methods)

When DIY Makes Sense

DIY pressure washing is reasonable for small, hard surfaces only: a single walkway, a small concrete patio, or a section of fence. These are low-risk jobs where a rented machine at 2,000 PSI with a 25-degree nozzle is unlikely to cause damage.

When to Hire a Pro

Hire a professional for anything involving your home's exterior siding, roof, second-story surfaces, large driveways, or surfaces near landscaping. In Greenville's climate, most homes need soft washing for siding — which requires specialized equipment and chemical knowledge that rental machines do not provide.

Greenville-Specific Advice

Greenville's red clay soil stains concrete in ways that consumer machines struggle to remove. Professional surface cleaners with hot water capability cut through clay staining in one pass. A rented cold-water unit may leave you scrubbing for hours with mediocre results. If your driveway has orange-red clay streaks, skip the rental and call a pro.

Which Is Right for You?

If you value your time and want guaranteed results without risk of property damage, hiring a professional is the better investment. If you enjoy hands-on work and only need to clean a small concrete surface, DIY can save you $100-$200.
