Pressure Washing Cost Guide for Greenville, SC

What does pressure washing cost in Greenville, SC? Here is a detailed pricing guide so you know what to expect before calling for quotes.

Average Pressure Washing Prices in Greenville

  • House washing (1,500-2,500 sq ft): $200-$500
  • Driveway (2-car): $100-$250
  • Deck or patio: $150-$350
  • Fence (per linear foot): $1-$3
  • Roof soft wash: $300-$600
  • Commercial building exterior: $0.10-$0.30 per sq ft

What Affects Pricing

  • Square footage — larger surfaces cost more but the per-sq-ft rate drops
  • Surface type — concrete, vinyl, wood, and brick all require different pressures and techniques
  • Height and access — two-story homes require ladders or extension wands
  • Condition — heavily stained or algae-covered surfaces take longer
  • Method — soft washing (low pressure + chemical) costs more than standard pressure washing but is required for certain surfaces

Soft Wash vs Pressure Wash Costs

Standard pressure washing uses high PSI water to blast dirt off hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure with cleaning solutions for delicate surfaces like vinyl siding, roofs, and painted wood. Soft washing typically costs 15-25% more due to chemical costs.

How to Get the Best Price

  • 1. Bundle services — house + driveway + deck is cheaper than booking three separate visits
  • 2. Schedule off-season — winter and early spring rates may be lower
  • 3. Maintain regularly — annual washing costs less than letting buildup accumulate for years
  • 4. Get written quotes — compare scope, not just price

Frequency Savings: Why Regular Maintenance Costs Less

One of the biggest pricing factors is how long it has been since the last cleaning. A home that gets annual pressure washing is a faster, easier job than one that has gone 3-5 years without service.

Here is how the math works:

  • Annual cleaning: $250-$400 per visit. Surfaces are lightly soiled with no heavy buildup. A crew can wash your home in 1-2 hours.
  • Every 2-3 years: $350-$500 per visit. Moderate algae and grime require pre-treatment and extra dwell time. Takes 2-3 hours.
  • 5+ years of buildup: $500-$700+. Heavy organic growth, potential permanent staining. May need multiple passes or specialized treatments. Takes 3-5 hours.

The annual customer pays $250-$400 per year and always has a clean home. The homeowner who waits 5 years pays $500-$700 once and deals with permanent staining, potential surface damage, and years of reduced curb appeal in the meantime.

Over a 5-year period:

  • Annual plan: 5 cleanings at $300 average = $1,500 total, clean home every month of every year
  • One-time deep clean at year 5: $600 + 4 years of dirty surfaces + harder-to-remove stains

Regular maintenance also prevents the kind of damage that leads to expensive repairs — mold behind siding, rotting wood, cracked concrete from freeze-thaw cycles in trapped moisture. Spending $300 per year on washing can prevent $2,000-$5,000 in repairs down the line.

Insurance and Damage Prevention

Pressure washing is one of the most cost-effective ways to protect your Greenville property from long-term damage. Here is what unchecked grime actually does to your home:

Structural damage from organic growth:

  • Mold and mildew on wood siding, trim, and deck boards break down wood fibers over time. What starts as a cosmetic issue becomes wood rot requiring replacement at $500-$3,000 per affected area.
  • Algae on concrete traps moisture. In SC's climate, that moisture causes spalling (surface flaking) and accelerates crack formation.
  • Moss on roofs lifts shingle edges and creates water entry points. A single leak can cause $1,000-$10,000 in interior water damage.

Slip-and-fall liability:

  • Algae-covered walkways and patios are a slip-and-fall hazard. If a guest or delivery person is injured on your property, homeowner's insurance typically covers the claim — but your premiums may increase 10-25% after a claim.
  • Regular cleaning of walkways, driveways, and porches eliminates this risk for a fraction of what one claim would cost.

Insurance and warranty considerations:

  • Some homeowner's insurance policies require reasonable property maintenance. Visible neglect — heavy mold, moss damage, deteriorated surfaces — can complicate a claim.
  • Roof shingle manufacturers often require that algae and moss be addressed to maintain warranty coverage. A $400 soft wash protects a $10,000-$15,000 roof warranty.
  • If you are selling your home, inspection reports will flag organic growth on exterior surfaces. Addressing it proactively avoids last-minute negotiations and potential price reductions.

HOA compliance:

  • Many Greenville HOAs include exterior cleanliness in their CC&Rs. Violations can result in fines of $50-$200 per month until the issue is resolved. An annual pressure wash keeps you in compliance year-round.

Pressure Washing Cost FAQ

How much does it cost to pressure wash a 2,000 sq ft house in Greenville?

Most Greenville companies charge $250-$450 for a standard 2,000 sq ft home. This includes the full exterior — siding, trim, soffits, and a final rinse of windows.

Is soft washing more expensive than pressure washing?

Yes, typically 15-25% more. Soft washing uses specialized cleaning solutions and requires more product per job. However, it is the only safe method for vinyl siding, roofs, painted surfaces, and stucco. The extra cost prevents damage that would cost far more to repair.

Can I negotiate the price?

The best way to lower your per-service cost is to bundle multiple surfaces in one visit or sign up for an annual maintenance plan. Most companies, including Greenville Pressure Pros, offer package discounts rather than line-item negotiation.

Should I always go with the cheapest quote?

Not necessarily. Verify that the quote includes all surfaces, proper chemicals, and insurance coverage. A company quoting $150 for a house wash may be using incorrect pressure settings or skipping pre-treatment — both of which can lead to damage or poor results. Ask what equipment and method they use for each surface.

Are there extra fees I should watch for?

Ask upfront about second-story surcharges, heavy stain pre-treatment fees, water supply charges if you do not have a working exterior spigot, and travel fees if you are outside the company's core service area.
