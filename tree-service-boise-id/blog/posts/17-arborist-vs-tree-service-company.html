Arborist vs Tree Service Company in Boise

Certified Arborist

Certified Arborist costs $150-$500 consultation in Boise. ISA certified, diagnosis and treatment plans, health focus. This option works best for homeowners who want reliable results and are willing to invest in quality.

For most Boise projects, certified arborist is the right choice when you need professional-grade results that will last. Boise Tree Crew can help you determine if this is the best fit for your specific situation.

Tree Service Company

Tree Service Company runs $150-$3,500 per job in Boise. Equipment and crew, removal/trimming/grinding, physical work. This is a solid option when budget is a priority or when the project scope is smaller.

Many Boise homeowners start here and upgrade later if needed. The key is matching the solution to the problem — not every job requires the premium option.

Which Is Right for You?

  • Choose Certified Arborist when you need professional results, warranties, and long-term value.
  • Choose Tree Service Company when budget is the priority or the project is smaller in scope.
