Tree Service FAQ for Boise Homeowners

Common questions about tree service in Boise, ID. Call 208-555-0126 if you need answers not covered here.

How much does tree removal cost in Boise?

It depends on the tree's size, location, and condition. Small trees (under 30 ft) run $200-$500. Medium trees are $500-$1,200. Large trees near structures can be $1,200-$2,500+. We always provide a free on-site estimate with an exact price.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Boise?

It depends on the tree species, size, and your neighborhood. Some Boise areas have tree protection ordinances. Boise Tree Crew can advise you during the estimate and help with permit applications if needed.

How long does tree removal take?

Most single-tree removals take 2-4 hours. Large trees near structures that require rigging or a crane can take a full day. We give you a time estimate during the on-site quote.

Do you remove the stump too?

Stump grinding is typically a separate line item. We can do it the same day as the removal or schedule it separately. Stump grinding runs $100-$400 depending on size.

What happens to the wood?

We chip branches on site. Trunk sections can be left for firewood if you want them, otherwise we haul everything away. Large logs from valuable species can sometimes be donated to local woodworkers.

Is tree work covered by insurance?

Homeowner insurance typically covers tree removal when a tree falls on a structure. It usually does not cover elective removal of healthy or dying trees. We recommend checking your policy before scheduling.

When is the best time for tree trimming?

The dormant season (late fall through early spring) is ideal for most species. Dead or hazardous branches should be removed immediately regardless of season.

How far in advance should I book?

For non-emergency work, booking 1-2 weeks ahead is typical. During storm season, availability tightens. Emergency calls are handled the same day.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — always. Call 208-555-0126 and we will schedule a free on-site assessment.

Boise-Specific Tree Service Tips

A few things unique to tree care in the Boise area:

  • Watering stressed trees — Boise's dry summers stress trees, especially those not on irrigation. Deep watering once a week during July and August is more effective than frequent shallow watering. A stressed tree is more vulnerable to disease and storm damage.
  • Fire-safe landscaping in the Foothills — if your property is in the Boise Foothills wildfire interface zone, maintain defensible space: trim lower branches to 6-10 feet above ground, keep canopy at least 10 feet from structures and other trees, and remove dead wood promptly.
  • Cottonwood management — cottonwoods are the most common problem tree in the Boise area. They grow fast, drop massive amounts of cotton in spring, have aggressive roots, and are prone to branch failure. Regular trimming is essential if you choose to keep them.
  • Wind events — Boise gets strong Chinook winds in winter and spring that can exceed 60 mph. Trees with dense canopies, poor structure, or root damage are most vulnerable. Preventive trimming before wind season reduces risk significantly.
  • City tree requirements — Boise City requires homeowners to maintain sidewalk and street clearance for trees on their property. If your tree blocks the sidewalk (below 8 feet) or street (below 14 feet), you can receive a notice from the city to trim it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work in winter?

Yes. Winter is actually an excellent time for tree work in Boise. Frozen ground reduces lawn damage from equipment, bare trees allow better visibility of the branch structure, and scheduling is more flexible. The only limitation is extreme cold or ice that creates safety hazards for the crew.

Can you work around power lines?

We can trim branches near power lines following OSHA safety standards, maintaining required clearance distances. For branches that are in direct contact with power lines, Idaho Power must be involved to de-energize the lines before work can proceed. We coordinate with Idaho Power when needed — this is a standard part of the process for many Boise neighborhoods with older trees.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Boise and surrounding areas including Eagle, Meridian, Nampa, Caldwell, Star, Kuna, and Garden City. Travel fees may apply for locations more than 20 miles from our base.
