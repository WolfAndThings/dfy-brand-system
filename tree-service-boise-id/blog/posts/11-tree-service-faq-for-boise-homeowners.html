Tree Service FAQ for Boise Homeowners

Common questions about tree service in Boise, ID. Call 208-555-0126 if you need answers not covered here.

How much does tree removal cost in Boise?

It depends on the tree's size, location, and condition. Small trees (under 30 ft) run $200-$500. Medium trees are $500-$1,200. Large trees near structures can be $1,200-$2,500+. We always provide a free on-site estimate with an exact price.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Boise?

It depends on the tree species, size, and your neighborhood. Some Boise areas have tree protection ordinances. Boise Tree Crew can advise you during the estimate and help with permit applications if needed.

How long does tree removal take?

Most single-tree removals take 2-4 hours. Large trees near structures that require rigging or a crane can take a full day. We give you a time estimate during the on-site quote.

Do you remove the stump too?

Stump grinding is typically a separate line item. We can do it the same day as the removal or schedule it separately. Stump grinding runs $100-$400 depending on size.

What happens to the wood?

We chip branches on site. Trunk sections can be left for firewood if you want them, otherwise we haul everything away. Large logs from valuable species can sometimes be donated to local woodworkers.

Is tree work covered by insurance?

Homeowner insurance typically covers tree removal when a tree falls on a structure. It usually does not cover elective removal of healthy or dying trees. We recommend checking your policy before scheduling.

When is the best time for tree trimming?

The dormant season (late fall through early spring) is ideal for most species. Dead or hazardous branches should be removed immediately regardless of season.

How far in advance should I book?

For non-emergency work, booking 1-2 weeks ahead is typical. During storm season, availability tightens. Emergency calls are handled the same day.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — always. Call 208-555-0126 and we will schedule a free on-site assessment.
