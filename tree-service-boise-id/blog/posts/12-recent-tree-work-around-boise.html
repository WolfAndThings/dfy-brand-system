Recent Tree Work Around Boise

Here is a look at recent tree work completed by Boise Tree Crew across Boise and surrounding areas.

Large Oak Removal — Boise Residential

A 70-foot red oak had developed a significant lean toward the homeowner's garage after years of root erosion from a nearby creek. Our crew used a crane to section the tree from the top down, with a ground crew managing debris and traffic control. Stump was ground to 10 inches below grade. Total job time: 6 hours.

Storm Damage Cleanup — Boise Suburbs

After severe thunderstorms, a mature pine split and dropped a 40-foot section onto the homeowner's fence and part of their deck. We arrived within 3 hours of the call, cleared the fallen section, trimmed the remaining trunk to a safe point, and tarped the damaged deck area. Insurance covered the full removal.

Hedge and Overgrowth Trimming — Boise HOA

An HOA with 24 units hired us to trim all overgrown trees and hedges along the property perimeter. The job included 30+ trees trimmed to clearance specifications, hedge shaping, and full debris removal. Completed over 3 days with zero disruption to residents.

Stump Grinding x12 — New Development Site

A contractor clearing a residential lot needed 12 stumps ground after the initial tree clearing. We completed all 12 stumps in one day, grinding each to 8 inches below grade and filling with wood chip mulch.

Emergency Pine Removal — After Ice Storm

An ice-loaded pine fell across a driveway, trapping two vehicles. We responded within 2 hours, sectioned the tree, cleared the driveway, and removed all debris. The homeowner had access restored before sunset.
