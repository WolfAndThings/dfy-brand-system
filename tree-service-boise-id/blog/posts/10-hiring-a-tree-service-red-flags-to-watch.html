Hiring a Tree Service: Red Flags to Watch

Not all tree services in Boise are equal. Here are the red flags to watch for when hiring, and what to look for in a reputable company.

Red Flags to Avoid

  • 1. No insurance — if a worker gets hurt on your property or damages your home, you could be liable. Always ask for proof of general liability AND workers compensation.
  • 2. Door-to-door solicitation — legitimate tree companies do not knock on doors offering cheap work. This is a common scam, especially after storms.
  • 3. Cash-only, no contract — reputable companies provide written estimates and accept multiple payment methods.
  • 4. Topping trees — any company that suggests cutting the top off a tree is using outdated, harmful practices. Topping kills trees slowly and creates hazardous regrowth.
  • 5. No references or reviews — check Google reviews, Yelp, and ask for local references.
  • 6. Drastically low bids — if one quote is half the others, something is wrong. They may be cutting corners on safety, insurance, or disposal.
  • 7. Demanding full payment upfront — standard practice is no deposit or a small deposit, with the balance due on completion.
  • 8. No cleanup included — the quote should include all debris removal, chipping, and site cleanup.

What to Look For

  • Current insurance — ask for certificates, not just verbal confirmation
  • ISA certification — International Society of Arboriculture certification shows training and competence
  • Written estimates — detailed scope of work, not just a number
  • Proper equipment — professional crews have chippers, bucket trucks, and rigging gear
  • Local reputation — established companies with reviews from Boise customers
  • Clean trucks and equipment — a sign the company takes its work seriously

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

  • 1. Can I see your insurance certificates?
  • 2. Are your climbers ISA certified?
  • 3. Does the price include stump grinding?
  • 4. How will you protect my lawn and landscaping?
  • 5. What happens if you damage my property?
  • 6. How long will the job take?
