Lot Clearing for New Construction in Boise

Planning new construction or a major landscaping project in Boise? Lot clearing removes trees, brush, and debris to prepare the site. Here is what the process looks like.

What Lot Clearing Includes

  • Removing all trees and stumps on the designated area
  • Clearing brush, shrubs, and undergrowth
  • Grubbing roots from the soil
  • Grading the cleared area for drainage
  • Hauling all debris off site
  • Chipping usable wood for mulch

When You Need Lot Clearing

  • New home construction on a wooded lot
  • Adding a garage, shop, or accessory dwelling unit
  • Installing a driveway or parking area
  • Creating usable yard space from overgrown areas
  • Preparing land for fencing or retaining walls
  • Fire safety clearance in wildfire-prone areas

The Lot Clearing Process

  • 1. Survey and marking — identify property lines, setbacks, and trees to preserve
  • 2. Permit check — Boise may require tree removal permits for certain species or sizes
  • 3. Equipment staging — chainsaws, chippers, excavators, and dump trailers
  • 4. Tree felling — take down trees in the clearing zone
  • 5. Stump grinding — grind all stumps below grade
  • 6. Brush clearing — remove all shrubs, vines, and undergrowth
  • 7. Debris hauling — chip branches, haul logs, and remove all waste
  • 8. Rough grading — level the site for the next phase of construction

Cost Factors

Lot clearing costs in Boise depend on:

  • Lot size and tree density
  • Tree species and sizes
  • Access for heavy equipment
  • Disposal requirements
  • Grading needs

Typical ranges:

  • Light clearing (sparse trees, small lot): $1,500-$3,000
  • Moderate clearing (half-acre, mixed trees): $3,000-$6,000
  • Heavy clearing (dense wooded acre+): $6,000-$15,000+
