DIY Removal

DIY Removal costs $50-$200 saw rental in Boise. Extreme injury risk, no insurance, only for small trees under 15 ft. This option works best for homeowners who want reliable results and are willing to invest in quality.

For most Boise projects, diy removal is the right choice when you need professional-grade results that will last. Boise Tree Crew can help you determine if this is the best fit for your specific situation.

Professional Removal

Professional Removal runs $300-$3,500 full service in Boise. Insured and trained crew, any size tree, liability covered. This is a solid option when budget is a priority or when the project scope is smaller.

Many Boise homeowners start here and upgrade later if needed. The key is matching the solution to the problem — not every job requires the premium option.

Which Is Right for You?

  • Choose DIY Removal when you need professional results, warranties, and long-term value.
  • Choose Professional Removal when budget is the priority or the project is smaller in scope.
