Storm Damage Tree Cleanup in Boise

After storms hit Boise, damaged trees become an immediate safety hazard. Here is what to do first and how to get professional storm cleanup fast.

Immediate Steps After Storm Damage

  • 1. Stay away from downed trees near power lines — call your utility company first
  • 2. Do not go under hanging branches — partially broken limbs can fall without warning
  • 3. Document the damage — take photos from a safe distance for insurance claims
  • 4. Check your roof — look for punctures or damage from fallen branches
  • 5. Call your insurance company — most homeowner policies cover storm damage tree removal

When to Call Emergency Tree Service

Call immediately if:

  • A tree has fallen on your house, car, fence, or power line
  • Large branches are hanging over walkways or driveways
  • A tree is leaning and could fall in the next storm
  • Debris is blocking your driveway or road access

What Storm Cleanup Includes

  • Hazard removal — cutting and removing fallen trees and hanging limbs
  • Debris chipping — branches are fed through a chipper on site
  • Log removal — trunk sections are cut and hauled away
  • Stump grinding — optional, can be done at the same visit or scheduled later
  • Roof tarping — if a tree punctured your roof, we can provide temporary protection

Insurance and Storm Damage

Most homeowner insurance policies cover:

  • Tree removal when a tree falls on a structure (house, garage, fence, shed)
  • Debris removal from blocked driveways or walkways
  • Temporary repairs to prevent further damage

Policies typically do NOT cover:

  • Tree removal when the tree falls in the yard and does not hit anything
  • Pre-existing dead trees the homeowner knew about
  • Cosmetic cleanup of branches and leaves

Post-Storm Tree Assessment

After the immediate hazard is cleared, have a professional assess your remaining trees. Storm-stressed trees may have hidden damage:

  • Cracked branch unions that could fail in the next wind event
  • Root heaving from saturated soil
  • Bark damage from flying debris
