What Affects Your Tree Removal Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Tree height (under 30 ft vs over 60 ft)
  • Trunk diameter at chest height
  • Proximity to structures, fences, or power lines
  • Whether crane access is required
  • Stump grinding included or separate

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Tree Removal in Boise

  • Get quotes from 2-3 companies and compare scope, not just price — make sure stump grinding and cleanup are included
  • Schedule removal in winter (dormant season) when crews have more availability and pricing is lower
  • Ask if the wood can be left as firewood — this may reduce your hauling cost
  • Combine tree removal with stump grinding in one visit for a package discount
