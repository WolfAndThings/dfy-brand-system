What Affects Your Tree Health Assessment Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Number of trees being assessed
  • Whether lab testing or soil sampling is needed
  • Size and species of trees
  • Whether a written report is required
  • Travel distance to the property

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Tree Health in Boise

  • Start with a visual inspection before committing to lab tests or soil sampling
  • Ask your arborist about free follow-up guidance calls after the assessment
  • Combine health assessments for multiple trees in one visit for a better rate
  • Many tree health issues caught early cost far less to treat than they would to remove the tree later
