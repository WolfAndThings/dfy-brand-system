People Also Ask

Common questions about tree service in Boise.

How much does tree removal cost in Boise?+
Tree removal in Boise costs $300 to $3,500 depending on tree size, location, and complexity. Small trees under 30 feet run $300-$700. Large trees over 60 feet cost $1,500-$3,500. Call 208-555-0126 for a free estimate.
Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Boise?+
Boise requires permits for removing trees over 6 inches in diameter on residential property, and all trees in the public right-of-way. Boise Tree Crew handles permit applications as part of our service.
What's the best time of year to trim trees in Boise?+
Late winter (February-March) is ideal for most tree trimming in Boise — trees are dormant, you can see the structure, and spring growth seals cuts quickly. Avoid heavy pruning in fall when trees are storing energy.
How far should trees be from my house?+
Large trees should be at least 20 feet from your Boise home, medium trees 15 feet, and small trees 10 feet. Branches overhanging your roof should be trimmed back to prevent damage during Idaho wind and snow storms.
