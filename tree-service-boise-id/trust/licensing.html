Licensed & Insured Tree Service — Boise Tree Crew

Tree work is one of the most dangerous trades in the country. Chainsaws, climbing, rigging, heavy wood — the potential for property damage and personal injury is real. Hiring a licensed, insured tree company is not just smart — it is essential. Here is what Boise Tree Crew carries.

Our Licenses & Certifications

  • State contractor license — registered and in good standing with ID contractor licensing board
  • City business license — licensed to operate in Boise and surrounding municipalities
  • ISA Certified Arborist — International Society of Arboriculture certified arborists on staff for assessments, pruning plans, and health evaluations
  • TCIA member — Tree Care Industry Association membership, demonstrating commitment to industry safety standards
  • Pesticide applicator license — licensed for tree injection treatments and pest management applications as required by ID

Insurance Coverage

  • General liability insurance — minimum \$2M per occurrence. Covers damage to your home, fence, landscape, driveway, or any other property damaged during tree work. This is critical — a falling tree section can cause serious damage even with experienced crews.
  • Workers compensation insurance — covers every crew member. Tree work injuries happen. Without workers comp, a homeowner can be held liable for medical bills if an uninsured worker is hurt on their property.
  • Commercial auto insurance — covers our trucks, chippers, and equipment during transit and operation.
  • Completed operations coverage — covers issues that arise after the job is done, such as a stump grinding settling that damages underground utilities.

Why This Matters to You

The tree service industry has a high rate of unlicensed operators. A guy with a truck and a chainsaw is not a tree service — he is a liability. Unlicensed tree workers cause more property damage, have higher injury rates, and often leave when a job goes wrong. If an uninsured climber falls on your property, you could face a six-figure lawsuit.

Boise Tree Crew carries full insurance because we respect both our crew and your property. Every job starts with verified coverage.
