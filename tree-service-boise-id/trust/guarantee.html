Our Tree Service Guarantee — Boise Tree Crew

Tree work is permanent — you cannot put a branch back. That is why Boise Tree Crew guarantees quality, safety, and professionalism on every single job in Boise. Here is exactly what you can expect.

The Boise Tree Crew Guarantee

Safe, Professional Execution

Every job is planned before the first cut. We assess the tree, the surroundings, the drop zone, and any obstacles. We use proper rigging, roping, and equipment for every situation. Climbing spikes are never used on trees being preserved. We follow ISA and ANSI A300 pruning standards on every trim.

Property Protection

We protect your lawn, driveway, landscaping, and structures during tree work. Equipment is placed on plywood or mats. Drop zones are cleared and monitored. If our work damages any part of your property, we repair or replace it at our expense — no arguments.

Complete Debris Cleanup

Every job ends with a thorough cleanup. Branches are chipped, logs are hauled or stacked per your preference, and the site is raked clean. You will not find a single twig left behind. The yard should look better than before we arrived — minus the tree.

Honest Assessments

If a tree does not need to come down, we will tell you. If pruning can solve the problem, we recommend pruning. We do not upsell removals when cabling, pruning, or monitoring is the right call. Our arborists give honest recommendations based on tree health and your safety — not our revenue.

100% Satisfaction

If any aspect of our work does not meet your expectations, tell us before we leave and we fix it immediately. If you notice an issue after, call within 48 hours and we will return at no additional cost to address it.

What Is NOT Covered

  • Natural settling of stump grinding areas over time (we fill with grindings but settling is normal)
  • Regrowth from stumps not treated with herbicide (available on request)
  • Damage caused by subsequent storms to trees we have pruned (pruning reduces risk but does not eliminate it)

How to File a Concern

Call 208-555-0126 and ask for the owner or project manager. We take every concern seriously and aim to resolve issues within 24-48 hours. Photos are helpful if describing property damage or incomplete work.
