Emergency Tree Service in Boise, ID

Tree down? Hanging over your house? Do not wait. Boise Tree Crew provides 24/7 emergency tree service across Boise. We dispatch within hours for fallen trees, storm damage, and hazardous limbs threatening structures and safety.

When to Call for Emergency Tree Service

  • Fallen tree on a structure — tree has landed on your roof, garage, fence, vehicle, or any building
  • Storm damage with hanging limbs — large branches cracked but still attached, hanging over your home, driveway, or walkway
  • Leaning tree threatening a structure — sudden lean change after a storm, visible root plate lifting, or trunk crack indicating imminent failure
  • Tree on power lines or utility lines — call your utility company first for live wires, then call us for the tree once lines are de-energized
  • Blocked roads or driveways — fallen tree preventing access to your property or blocking a road
  • Split trunk in a storm — co-dominant stems or V-crotch failure where half the tree has separated and the other half is unstable

Response Time

For trees on structures or blocking access, we dispatch within 1-3 hours during daylight. After dark, we assess by phone and often dispatch at first light unless the situation involves immediate danger to people. Weekend and holiday emergency service is always available — storms do not wait for Monday and neither do we.

What to Expect

  1. Call immediately — describe the situation, send photos if safe to do so, and let us know if anyone is in danger or if the tree has damaged utilities. We triage and prioritize.
  2. Crew dispatch with proper equipment — we send the right crew with the right gear: chainsaws, rigging, crane when needed, and chipper. No second trips.
  3. Safe removal and stabilization — our crew removes the hazard, stabilizes any remaining structure risk, and clears debris from the impact area.
  4. Full cleanup and documentation — we chip or haul all debris, take photos for your insurance claim, and leave the site safe and clean.

After-Hours Availability

Our emergency line is monitored 24/7, including weekends and holidays. If a tree has hit your house at 2 AM during an ice storm, call us. We will assess over the phone, advise on immediate safety steps, and have a crew out as soon as conditions allow safe work. If there is immediate danger to occupants, call 911 first — then call us.
