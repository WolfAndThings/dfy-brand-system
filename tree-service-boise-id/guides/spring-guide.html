Spring Tree Care Guide for Boise, ID

Spring is the most important season for tree health in Boise. As dormancy breaks and new growth begins, this is the window to prune, inspect for storm damage, plant new trees, and catch disease before it spreads. Getting ahead of spring growth saves money and prevents problems all year.

Your Spring Tree Service Checklist

  • Pruning before spring growth starts
  • Storm damage cleanup from winter
  • New tree planting and transplanting
  • Disease and pest inspection
  • Deadwood removal from canopy
  • Spring fertilization and soil assessment

Spring Pruning Window

The best time to prune most tree species in Boise is late winter through early spring — just before new growth starts. This timing allows the tree to heal quickly as sap flow increases, and you can see the branch structure clearly before leaves fill in. Once full leaf-out happens, structural issues become invisible until something breaks.

Post-Winter Storm Damage

Winter storms leave cracked limbs, split trunks, and hanging branches throughout Boise neighborhoods. These are not just ugly — they are hazards that will fall eventually, usually at the worst possible time. Spring is the time to walk your property, look up, and call for anything that looks damaged, cracked, or dead.

Disease Inspection Season

Spring is when fungal diseases and insect infestations become visible. Cankers, unusual leaf patterns, bore holes, sap bleeding, and mushroom growth at the base are all signs that need professional assessment. Early detection often means treatment instead of removal.

Spring Planting

If you removed a tree last year or want to add to your landscape, spring is the ideal planting window. Soil temperatures are rising, rain is plentiful, and new trees have a full growing season to establish roots before winter.
