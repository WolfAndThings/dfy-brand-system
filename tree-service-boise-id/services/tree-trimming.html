Frequently Asked Questions

How much does tree trimming cost in Boise?

Tree trimming in Boise costs $200 to $1,000 per tree depending on size and condition. Small ornamental trees start around $150, while large shade trees with extensive canopy cost more. Call 208-555-0126 for a free estimate.

How often should trees be trimmed in Boise?

Most trees in Boise should be trimmed every 3 to 5 years to maintain health and shape. Fast-growing species may need trimming every 1 to 2 years. Fruit trees are typically pruned annually for the best production.

When is the best time to trim trees in Boise?

Late winter to early spring, while trees are dormant, is the ideal trimming time for most species in Boise. However, dead or hazardous branches should be removed immediately regardless of season. Boise Tree Crew trims year-round.

Will trimming hurt my tree?

Proper trimming by a certified arborist does not harm your tree. Boise Tree Crew follows ISA pruning standards and never removes more than 25 percent of the canopy at one time. Topping or lion-tailing damages trees and we do not practice either technique.

Do you clean up after tree trimming?

Yes. Boise Tree Crew includes full cleanup with every tree trimming job in Boise. We chip branches, rake the area, and haul away all debris. Your yard is left clean when we are finished.
