How often should hedges be trimmed in Boise?

Most hedges in Boise should be trimmed 2 to 3 times per year to maintain a neat shape. Fast-growing hedges like privet may need monthly trimming during the growing season. Boise Tree Crew offers recurring service plans.

When is the best time to trim hedges?

The best time to trim most hedges in Boise is late spring after the first flush of new growth, and again in late summer. Avoid trimming in late fall as new growth may not harden off before winter.

Can you reshape overgrown hedges?

Yes. Boise Tree Crew reshapes overgrown hedges in Boise by cutting back to the desired shape over one or two sessions. Severely overgrown hedges may need a hard renovation cut and time to regrow.

Do you haul away the hedge trimmings?

Yes. Boise Tree Crew includes full cleanup and debris removal with every hedge trimming job in Boise. We bag or chip the trimmings and leave your property clean.
