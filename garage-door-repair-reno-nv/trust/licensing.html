Licensed & Insured Garage Door Service — Reno Garage Door Pros

Garage door systems operate under extreme spring tension and involve electrical components, heavy panels, and moving parts that can cause serious injury if mishandled. Hiring a licensed and insured garage door company protects you and your property. Here is what Reno Garage Door Pros carries.

Our Licenses & Credentials

  • State contractor license — registered and in good standing with NV contractor licensing board
  • City business license — licensed to operate in Reno and surrounding municipalities
  • Manufacturer-authorized dealer — authorized installer and service provider for major garage door and opener manufacturers
  • IDA member — International Door Association membership, demonstrating commitment to industry training and safety standards
  • Electrical certification — qualified for opener installation, wiring, and smart-home integration as required by local code

Insurance Coverage

  • General liability insurance — minimum \$1M per occurrence. Covers damage to your vehicle, garage interior, driveway, or any property affected during service. A falling garage door panel or a spring under tension can cause significant damage — our insurance covers it.
  • Workers compensation insurance — garage door springs are one of the most dangerous components in residential service work. Every Reno Garage Door Pros technician is covered for on-the-job injuries.
  • Commercial auto insurance — covers our service vehicles and all parts and equipment carried on board.

Why This Matters to You

Garage door springs store enough energy to cause serious injury or death. Unlicensed operators who improperly install or adjust springs put your family at risk. They also void manufacturer warranties on doors and openers. If an uninsured tech is injured working on your door, you could be held liable for medical costs.

Reno Garage Door Pros technicians are trained, certified, and insured. We install to manufacturer specifications, maintain proper insurance coverage, and guarantee our work.
