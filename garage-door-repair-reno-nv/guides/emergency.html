Emergency Garage Door Service in Reno, NV

Garage door stuck? Spring broken? Do not force it. Reno Garage Door Pros provides same-day and emergency garage door repair across Reno. A broken garage door is a security risk and a safety hazard — call now and we dispatch fast.

When to Call for Emergency Garage Door Service

  • Broken spring — you heard a loud bang from the garage and the door will not open, or opens only a few inches. The spring has snapped and the door is extremely heavy. Do not attempt to operate it manually.
  • Door off track — the door has jumped its track and is hanging at an angle. It may fall unpredictably. Do not try to force it back into position.
  • Door will not close — the door opens but refuses to close, leaving your home and vehicles unsecured. Could be sensor, track, spring, or cable issue.
  • Door frozen to the ground — winter ice has bonded the bottom seal to the concrete and the opener cannot break it free
  • Snapped cable — a cable has broken and the door is hanging unevenly or has dropped on one side. Extremely dangerous — do not operate.
  • Vehicle trapped inside — you need your car and the garage door will not open. Time-sensitive and stressful — we prioritize these calls.

Response Time

For broken springs, off-track doors, and security-related failures, we dispatch same-day — typically within 2-4 hours during business hours. We carry the most common spring sizes, cables, rollers, and hardware on our service trucks so most repairs are completed in a single visit. Weekend and after-hours emergency service is available.

What to Expect

  1. Call and describe the problem — tell us what happened, what the door is doing (or not doing), and whether anyone or any vehicle is affected. We diagnose over the phone when possible.
  2. Technician dispatch — we send a tech with a fully stocked truck. Springs, cables, rollers, hinges, sensors, and opener parts are on board for immediate repair.
  3. On-site diagnosis and repair — the tech confirms the problem, gives you a price before starting, and completes the repair. Most emergency repairs take 30-90 minutes.
  4. Safety test and walkthrough — after repair, the tech tests the door through multiple cycles, checks safety reversals, and adjusts balance and force settings.

After-Hours Availability

Our standard hours are 7 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week. For genuine emergencies — door open and cannot close, vehicle trapped, security risk — we offer after-hours dispatch. Call the main line for after-hours service. We prioritize calls involving home security and safety.
