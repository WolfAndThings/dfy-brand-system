Repair vs Replace Your Garage Door: When to Decide

If you are comparing options for garage door repair in Reno, NV, this guide breaks down the key differences so you can make the right decision for your project and budget.

Quick Comparison

FactorRepairReplace
Cost$150-$600, same day service$1,000-$4,000, 1-2 day install
DetailsGood if door is under 15 years, keeps existing styleBest if door is 15+ years, new warranty and energy efficiency

Repair

Repair costs $150-$600, same day service in Reno. Good if door is under 15 years, keeps existing style. This option works best for homeowners who want reliable results and are willing to invest in quality.

For most Reno projects, repair is the right choice when you need professional-grade results that will last. Reno Garage Door Pros can help you determine if this is the best fit for your specific situation.

Replace

Replace runs $1,000-$4,000, 1-2 day install in Reno. Best if door is 15+ years, new warranty and energy efficiency. This is a solid option when budget is a priority or when the project scope is smaller.

Many Reno homeowners start here and upgrade later if needed. The key is matching the solution to the problem — not every job requires the premium option.

Which Is Right for You?

  • Choose Repair when you need professional results, warranties, and long-term value.
  • Choose Replace when budget is the priority or the project is smaller in scope.
