How much does garage door spring replacement cost in Reno?

How long does it take to replace garage door springs?

Spring replacement takes 45 minutes to 2 hours in most cases. Reno Garage Door Pros carries common spring sizes on our trucks so we can complete most Reno repairs in a single visit without needing to order parts.

How do I know if my garage door spring is broken?

Signs of a broken spring include the door not opening, a loud bang from the garage, a visible gap in the torsion spring, or the door feeling extremely heavy when lifted manually. If you see any of these in your Reno home, call Reno Garage Door Pros at 775-555-0243.

Is it safe to open my garage door with a broken spring?

No. Operating a garage door with a broken spring is dangerous and can damage the opener, cables, or door panels. If your spring breaks, do not try to force the door open. Call Reno Garage Door Pros for same-day spring replacement in Reno.

How long do garage door springs last?

Standard garage door springs last 7 to 12 years or about 10,000 cycles. High-cycle springs can last 20,000 or more cycles. Reno Garage Door Pros installs high-quality springs and can upgrade your Reno home to longer-lasting options.
