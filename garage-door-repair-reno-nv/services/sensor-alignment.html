Why does my garage door reverse when closing?

The most common cause is misaligned safety sensors. If the infrared beam between the two sensors at the bottom of the door is blocked or misaligned, the door reverses as a safety precaution. Reno Garage Door Pros fixes this quickly in Reno.

Can I align garage door sensors myself?

While basic sensor alignment is possible, incorrect adjustments can compromise safety. Reno Garage Door Pros uses precision tools to align sensors to manufacturer specifications for Reno homeowners, ensuring the safety system works correctly.

How do I know if my garage door sensors are bad?

Flashing sensor lights, the door reversing for no visible reason, or the door not closing at all are common signs. Check for obstructions first. If the problem persists, call Reno Garage Door Pros at 775-555-0243 for sensor diagnosis in Reno.

How long does sensor alignment take?

Sensor alignment in Reno takes 15 to 30 minutes in most cases. If the sensors are damaged rather than misaligned, replacement takes about 45 minutes. Reno Garage Door Pros carries replacement sensors for major brands on our trucks.
