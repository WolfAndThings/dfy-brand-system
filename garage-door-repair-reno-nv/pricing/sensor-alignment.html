What Affects Your Garage Door Sensor Alignment Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Whether sensors need replacement or just realignment
  • Wiring condition and length of run
  • Opener compatibility with sensor type
  • Whether the issue is sensor-related or logic board
  • Sun interference or obstruction diagnosis

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Sensor Alignment in Reno

  • Sensor issues are often caused by direct sunlight hitting the sensor — a simple sun shield can eliminate the problem for under $20
  • Check for spider webs, dirt, and debris on the sensor lens before calling for service — a quick wipe fixes many issues
  • If sensors were working fine and suddenly stopped, check for bumped or shifted mounting brackets before calling
  • Sensor alignment is one of the least expensive garage door service calls — do not let a company upsell you into an opener replacement for a sensor issue
