What Affects Your Garage Door Spring Replacement Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Spring type (torsion vs extension)
  • Number of springs being replaced
  • Door weight and size
  • Whether additional hardware needs replacement
  • Warranty on replacement springs

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Spring Replacement in Reno

  • Always replace both torsion springs at the same time even if only one broke — the second is the same age and will fail soon
  • Ask about high-cycle springs (25,000+ cycles) if you use your garage door 4+ times per day — they last 2-3 times longer
  • Never attempt spring replacement yourself — torsion springs are under extreme tension and cause serious injuries every year
  • Get quotes from 2-3 companies — spring replacement is a common upsell opportunity for unnecessary add-ons
