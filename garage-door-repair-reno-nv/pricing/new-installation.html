What Affects Your New Garage Door Installation Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Door size (single 8x7, double 16x7, custom)
  • Material type (steel, wood, composite, glass)
  • Insulation level (non-insulated, polystyrene, polyurethane)
  • Opener included or separate
  • Removal and disposal of old door

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on New Installation in Reno

  • Bundle opener installation with your new door for a package discount — most companies offer $50-$150 off the opener
  • Get insulated doors (R-12 or higher) if your garage is attached to your home — it makes a measurable difference in energy bills
  • Measure your existing opening carefully before shopping — non-standard sizes cost significantly more
  • Ask about manufacturer rebates and extended warranty programs — Clopay and Amarr frequently run promotions
