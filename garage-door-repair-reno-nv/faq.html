Garage Door Repair FAQ

Common questions about our Garage Door Repair services in Reno, NV.

How much does garage door repair cost?
Most repairs in Reno range from $150 to $400. Spring replacement is typically $200-$350 for a pair. New door installation starts around $800-$1,500.
Can you fix my door today?
Yes, most repairs are completed same-day. We carry common springs, rollers, cables, and opener parts on every truck.
Do you service all brands?
We service all major brands — LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman, Wayne Dalton, Clopay, and more.
Is it safe to use my door with a broken spring?
No. A broken spring means the full weight of the door is unsupported. Do not attempt to open or close it manually. Call us for same-day spring replacement.
Do you offer warranties?
Yes. We warranty our labor and the parts we install. Spring warranties are typically 1-3 years depending on the type.
How long do garage door springs last?
Standard springs last about 10,000 cycles (7-10 years of normal use). High-cycle springs last 25,000-50,000 cycles.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Fully licensed and insured with liability and workers comp coverage.
