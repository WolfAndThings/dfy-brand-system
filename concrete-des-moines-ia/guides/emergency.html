Emergency Concrete Repair in Des Moines, IA

Foundation crack growing? Sidewalk tripping hazard? Des Moines Concrete Team provides emergency concrete repair across Des Moines for situations that cannot wait. Structural cracks, safety hazards, and active water intrusion need immediate attention.

When to Call for Emergency Concrete Repair

  • Foundation cracks with water intrusion — water is entering your basement through a crack in the foundation wall. Every rain event makes it worse and mold risk increases daily.
  • Widening structural cracks — a foundation or structural crack is visibly growing, especially if accompanied by wall displacement or sticking doors and windows
  • Tripping hazard on public walkway — heaved or cracked sidewalk sections creating a liability and injury risk, especially on commercial property or rental units
  • Sudden slab settlement — a concrete slab has dropped or tilted suddenly, indicating possible void formation or soil erosion beneath the surface
  • Code violation deadline — city or HOA has issued a compliance notice requiring concrete repair within a specific timeframe
  • Vehicle or impact damage — a vehicle, dumpster, or equipment has damaged a driveway, curb, or foundation and the area needs stabilization

Response Time

For structural foundation concerns and active water intrusion, we schedule assessment within 24-48 hours. Tripping hazard repairs on commercial properties are prioritized for liability reasons. Emergency stabilization — crack injection, temporary leveling, and hazard marking — can often be completed same-day or next-day depending on scope.

What to Expect

  1. Call and describe the situation — tell us what you are seeing, when it started, and whether it is changing. Photos are extremely helpful for concrete issues — send them by text if possible.
  2. On-site assessment — our technician inspects the damage, measures crack width, checks for structural implications, and determines whether the issue requires emergency stabilization or can be scheduled for standard repair.
  3. Immediate stabilization if needed — for active water intrusion, we inject cracks with hydraulic cement or epoxy. For tripping hazards, we grind, level, or barricade. For structural concerns, we document and recommend engineering assessment if warranted.
  4. Permanent repair plan — we provide a written scope of work for permanent repair, including timeline and pricing. Insurance documentation provided if applicable.

After-Hours Availability

Concrete emergencies are rarely middle-of-the-night situations, but active foundation flooding and structural concerns warrant immediate attention. Call our main line and describe the urgency — we schedule priority assessments within 24 hours and can often respond faster for active water intrusion.
