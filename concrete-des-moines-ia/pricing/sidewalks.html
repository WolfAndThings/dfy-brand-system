What Affects Your Concrete Sidewalk Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Total linear feet and width
  • Whether existing sidewalk must be removed
  • Subgrade condition and preparation
  • ADA compliance requirements (slope, width)
  • Number of expansion joints and control cuts

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Sidewalks in Des Moines

  • Combine sidewalk with driveway work if both are needed — shared mobilization and concrete delivery lowers the per-square-foot cost
  • Ask your contractor about local code requirements for sidewalk width, thickness, and ADA compliance before pouring
  • Request broom finish perpendicular to the walking direction for the best slip resistance
  • Schedule sidewalk replacement before winter — freeze-thaw cycles will accelerate damage to cracked sections
