What Affects Your Stamped Concrete Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Total square footage
  • Pattern complexity (ashlar slate, cobblestone, wood plank)
  • Number of colors (base and accent)
  • Whether integral color, stain, or both are used
  • Sealer application included or separate

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Stamped Concrete in Des Moines

  • Stamped concrete must be sealed every 2-3 years to maintain its appearance — factor this maintenance cost into your decision
  • Ask to see completed projects in person, not just photos — stamped concrete quality varies dramatically between contractors
  • Choose a pattern and color that complements your home's exterior — the most popular patterns are ashlar slate and herringbone
  • Integral color (mixed into the concrete) lasts longer than surface-applied color — ask which method your contractor uses
