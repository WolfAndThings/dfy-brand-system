What Affects Your Garage Floor Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Garage size (1-car, 2-car, 3-car)
  • Whether existing floor must be removed
  • Floor coating or finish (broom, polished, epoxy-ready)
  • Thickness and reinforcement (standard 4 in vs 6 in)
  • Floor drain installation

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Garage Floors in Des Moines

  • If your existing garage floor is badly cracked or settling, replacement is usually more cost-effective than repair
  • Ask about thickened edges around the perimeter — this adds strength where the garage meets the driveway apron
  • Wait 28 days before applying epoxy or coating to a new garage floor — the concrete needs to fully cure
  • Consider a center floor drain if you wash cars in the garage or live in a climate with snow and road salt
