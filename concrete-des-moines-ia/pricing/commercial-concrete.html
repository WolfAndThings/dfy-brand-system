What Affects Your Commercial Concrete Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Total cubic yards of concrete
  • Project type (parking lot, building pad, loading dock)
  • Reinforcement and thickness specifications
  • Site preparation and forming complexity
  • Pour schedule and phasing requirements

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Commercial Concrete in Des Moines

  • Get quotes from contractors who specialize in commercial concrete — residential contractors may not have the equipment or experience
  • Ask about concrete mix design specifications (PSI, slump, aggregate) — commercial projects require higher performance concrete
  • Schedule large pours for early morning to allow maximum curing time before temperature drops
  • Request a phased pour schedule for large projects to maintain quality control and avoid cold joints
