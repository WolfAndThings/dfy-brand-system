Recent Concrete Projects in Des Moines

Here is a look at recent concrete projects completed by Des Moines Concrete Team across Des Moines and surrounding communities. These real jobs show the range of work we handle.

Driveway Replacement — Beaverdale

A homeowner in Beaverdale had a 30-year-old driveway with deep cracks, heaving, and surface scaling from decades of freeze-thaw cycles. We removed the old 4-inch slab, re-graded the base with 4 inches of compacted limestone, and poured a new 5-inch driveway with fiber mesh reinforcement. Control joints were cut at 10-foot intervals. Total project: 680 square feet, completed in 3 days. Cost range for this type of job: $5,500-$8,000.

Stamped Patio — West Des Moines

A couple in West Des Moines wanted a backyard patio for entertaining. We poured a 400 square foot stamped concrete patio with an Ashlar Slate pattern in a sandstone color with dark brown release. The patio included a 6-inch step-down from the back door and integrated drainage to direct water away from the foundation. Completed in 2 days plus cure time. Cost range: $4,000-$6,500.

Garage Floor Repair and Epoxy — Ankeny

A garage floor in Ankeny had significant spalling and surface damage from road salt exposure. We ground the surface, repaired cracks and low spots, and applied a two-coat epoxy system with decorative flake. The floor went from crumbling to showroom quality. Total time on site: 2 days. Cost range: $1,800-$3,000 for a 2-car garage.

Sidewalk Replacement — Downtown Des Moines

The City of Des Moines notified a property owner that their public sidewalk needed replacement due to trip hazards from tree root uplift. We removed 120 linear feet of sidewalk, cut problematic roots (with arborist approval), installed root barriers, and poured new 4-inch sidewalk with proper base. Project completed in 4 days. Cost range for sidewalk replacement: $8-$12 per square foot.

Foundation Repair — South Side

A 1960s ranch home on Des Moines' south side had horizontal cracking in the basement walls from lateral soil pressure — a common issue with Iowa's expansive clay soil. We installed carbon fiber straps to stabilize the walls and sealed all cracks with epoxy injection. The repair took 1 day and cost significantly less than wall replacement. Cost range for carbon fiber reinforcement: $300-$500 per strap installed.
