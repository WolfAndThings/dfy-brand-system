Concrete Patio vs Wood Deck in Des Moines

If you are comparing options for concrete in Des Moines, IA, this guide breaks down the key differences so you can make the right decision for your project and budget.

Concrete Patio

Concrete Patio costs $2,000-$8,000, 25+ year life in Des Moines. Minimal maintenance, handles snow and ice, many finish options. This option works best for homeowners who want reliable results and are willing to invest in quality.

For most Des Moines projects, concrete patio is the right choice when you need professional-grade results that will last. Des Moines Concrete Pros can help you determine if this is the best fit for your specific situation.

Wood Deck

Wood Deck runs $4,000-$15,000, 10-20 year life in Des Moines. Annual staining needed, vulnerable to rot, elevated views. This is a solid option when budget is a priority or when the project scope is smaller.

Many Des Moines homeowners start here and upgrade later if needed. The key is matching the solution to the problem — not every job requires the premium option.

Which Is Right for You?

  • Choose Concrete Patio when you need professional results, warranties, and long-term value.
  • Choose Wood Deck when budget is the priority or the project is smaller in scope.
