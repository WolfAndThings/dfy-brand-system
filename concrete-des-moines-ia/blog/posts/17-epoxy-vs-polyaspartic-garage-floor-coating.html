Epoxy vs Polyaspartic Garage Floor Coating

If you are comparing options for concrete in Des Moines, IA, this guide breaks down the key differences so you can make the right decision for your project and budget.

Quick Comparison

FactorEpoxy CoatingPolyaspartic Coating
Cost$3-$7/sq ft, 72-hour cure$5-$10/sq ft, 1-day cure
DetailsGood durability but yellows in UV exposure over timeSuperior durability, UV stable, ready to use next day

Epoxy Coating

Epoxy Coating costs $3-$7/sq ft, 72-hour cure in Des Moines. Good durability but yellows in UV exposure over time. This option works best for homeowners who want reliable results and are willing to invest in quality.

For most Des Moines projects, epoxy coating is the right choice when you need professional-grade results that will last. Des Moines Concrete Pros can help you determine if this is the best fit for your specific situation.

Polyaspartic Coating

Polyaspartic Coating runs $5-$10/sq ft, 1-day cure in Des Moines. Superior durability, UV stable, ready to use next day. This is a solid option when budget is a priority or when the project scope is smaller.

Many Des Moines homeowners start here and upgrade later if needed. The key is matching the solution to the problem — not every job requires the premium option.

Which Is Right for You?

  • Choose Epoxy Coating when you need professional results, warranties, and long-term value.
  • Choose Polyaspartic Coating when budget is the priority or the project is smaller in scope.
