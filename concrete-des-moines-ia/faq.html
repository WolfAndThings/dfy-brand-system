Concrete FAQ

Common questions about our Concrete services in Des Moines, IA.

How much does concrete work cost?
Concrete in Des Moines typically runs $8-$15 per square foot for standard flatwork. Stamped and decorative work runs $12-$25 per square foot. We provide free detailed quotes.
How long does a concrete driveway last?
A properly installed concrete driveway lasts 25-30 years or more with basic maintenance and occasional sealing.
Do you handle permits?
Yes. We pull any required permits and schedule inspections as needed for your project.
How long before I can drive on new concrete?
Light foot traffic in 24-48 hours. Vehicle traffic in 7 days. Full cure takes about 28 days.
Do you offer stamped concrete?
Yes — dozens of patterns and colors. Stone, brick, slate, tile, and wood plank designs are all available.
Can cracked concrete be repaired?
Often yes. Crack injection, mudjacking, and resurfacing can restore concrete at a fraction of replacement cost. We will tell you honestly which option fits.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Fully licensed and insured with liability and workers comp.
