Licensed & Insured Concrete Contractor — Des Moines Concrete Team

Concrete work is structural and permanent. A bad pour cannot be fixed — it has to be demolished and replaced. Hiring a licensed, insured concrete contractor ensures the job is done right the first time and that you are protected if anything goes wrong. Here is what Des Moines Concrete Team carries.

Our Licenses & Credentials

  • State contractor license — registered concrete contractor in good standing with IA licensing board
  • City business license — licensed to operate in Des Moines and surrounding municipalities
  • ACI certification — American Concrete Institute certified technicians for flatwork finishing, testing, and quality control
  • Building code compliance — all work meets or exceeds local building codes for reinforcement, thickness, drainage, and finish
  • Excavation and grading permits — properly permitted for site preparation, grading, and utility location as required

Insurance Coverage

  • General liability insurance — minimum \$2M per occurrence. Covers damage to your property, landscaping, underground utilities, and adjacent structures during concrete work. Excavation, forming, and pouring all carry risk — our insurance covers it completely.
  • Workers compensation insurance — concrete work is physically demanding and involves heavy equipment. Every Des Moines Concrete Team crew member is fully covered for on-the-job injuries.
  • Commercial auto insurance — covers our trucks, mixers, and equipment during transport and operation.
  • Completed operations coverage — covers issues that develop after the job is complete, such as structural cracking from improper reinforcement or drainage failures.

Why This Matters to You

Unlicensed concrete work is one of the most expensive mistakes a homeowner can make. A driveway poured too thin cracks within a year. A foundation without proper reinforcement fails under load. A patio without adequate base prep settles and heaves. And when the unlicensed contractor who did the work is gone, you pay for demolition and replacement — twice the cost of doing it right.

Des Moines Concrete Team is licensed, insured, and builds to code. We guarantee our work because we know it is done right.
