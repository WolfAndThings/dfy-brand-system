Our Concrete Work Guarantee — Des Moines Concrete Team

Concrete is permanent. You cannot undo a bad pour — you can only tear it out and start over. That is why Des Moines Concrete Team guarantees every concrete job in Des Moines is done to spec, on time, and built to last. Here is our commitment in plain language.

The Des Moines Concrete Team Guarantee

Structural Integrity

We guarantee proper mix design, reinforcement placement, base preparation, and curing for every pour. Concrete that cracks from improper technique, inadequate reinforcement, or insufficient base prep is our responsibility. We will repair or replace it at our cost.

Proper Finishing

Flatwork will be level, evenly finished, and properly graded for drainage. Stamped and decorative concrete will match the agreed-upon pattern and color. Control joints will be placed at proper intervals to manage natural cracking. If the finish does not match the specification, we make it right.

Site Cleanup

Concrete projects are messy — forms, rebar cutoffs, wire mesh scraps, washout, and equipment tracks. We clean up completely. Forms are removed on schedule, debris is hauled, and your yard is restored. Washout is contained and removed — never dumped on your property or down a drain.

Timeline Commitment

Weather permitting, we complete projects on the timeline we commit to. If rain or temperature delays are necessary to protect pour quality, we communicate immediately and reschedule at the earliest opportunity. We do not rush pours in bad conditions to stay on schedule — that is how bad concrete happens.

Workmanship Warranty

Des Moines Concrete Team provides a workmanship warranty on all concrete installations. Structural failures, finish defects, and drainage issues attributable to our work are covered. Call us and we fix it — no runaround.

What Is NOT Covered

  • Hairline surface cracks — these are normal in concrete and occur during the curing process. Control joints are designed to direct cracking to planned locations.
  • Damage from de-icing chemicals, vehicle impact, or heavy equipment placed on residential-grade concrete
  • Color variation due to natural curing conditions (temperature, humidity, and sunlight affect final color)
  • Settling caused by soil conditions below the base that were not visible during preparation

How to File a Concern

Call 515-555-0312 as soon as you notice an issue. Include photos and a description of the problem. We respond within 48 hours and schedule a site visit to assess. Structural concerns are prioritized and addressed immediately.
