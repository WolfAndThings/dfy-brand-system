Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a garage floor coating cost in Des Moines?

Garage floor concrete work in Des Moines ranges from $3 to $7 per square foot for a new slab, and $3 to $12 per square foot for coatings like epoxy or polyurea. A standard two-car garage runs $1,500 to $5,000. Call 515-555-0312 for a quote.

How long does a garage floor take to cure?

A new garage floor slab in Des Moines needs 7 days of curing before light use and 28 days before parking vehicles on it. Epoxy coatings typically cure in 24 to 72 hours depending on the product and temperature.

Can you fix cracks in my garage floor?

Yes. Des Moines Concrete Team repairs garage floor cracks, spalling, and surface damage in Des Moines. Small cracks can be filled with epoxy, while severely damaged floors may need a concrete overlay or full replacement. We assess the condition and recommend the best approach.

Should I get my garage floor coated or replaced?

If your garage floor in Des Moines is structurally sound with only surface damage, a coating is usually the better value. If the slab is heaving, settling, or has large cracks, replacement is the smarter long-term investment. Des Moines Concrete Team can advise after a free inspection.

How thick should a garage floor slab be?

A standard residential garage floor in Des Moines should be 4 inches thick with wire mesh or rebar reinforcement. If you plan to store heavy equipment or vehicles, Des Moines Concrete Team may recommend 5 to 6 inches with additional reinforcement.
