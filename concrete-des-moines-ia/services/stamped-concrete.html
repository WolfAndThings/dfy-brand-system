How much does stamped concrete cost in Des Moines?

Stamped concrete in Des Moines costs $10 to $18 per square foot depending on the pattern and number of colors. A 300-square-foot patio with a stone pattern runs approximately $3,000 to $5,400. Call 515-555-0312 for a free estimate.

How long does stamped concrete last?

Stamped concrete in Des Moines lasts 25 to 30 years with proper maintenance. Resealing every 2 to 3 years keeps the color vibrant and protects the surface from wear and weather damage.

What patterns are available for stamped concrete?

Des Moines Concrete Team offers dozens of stamped concrete patterns in Des Moines including flagstone, slate, brick, cobblestone, and wood plank. We bring samples to your home so you can see how each pattern looks with your house exterior.

Is stamped concrete slippery when wet?

Stamped concrete can be slippery when wet, especially with a glossy sealer. Des Moines Concrete Team uses non-slip additives in our sealers for all Des Moines projects, particularly around pools and walkways, to ensure safe traction.

Can stamped concrete be repaired if it chips?

Yes. Minor chips and wear on stamped concrete can be repaired with color-matched patching compound. Des Moines Concrete Team offers repair and reseal services in Des Moines to keep your stamped concrete looking like new.
