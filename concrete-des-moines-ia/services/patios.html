Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a concrete patio cost in Des Moines?

A concrete patio in Des Moines typically costs $2,000 to $6,000 depending on size and finish. A basic 12x12 slab starts around $2,000, while larger stamped or stained patios run higher. Call 515-555-0312 for a free estimate.

How long does a concrete patio take to install?

Most concrete patios in Des Moines are completed in 2 to 4 days including excavation, forming, pouring, and finishing. You should wait at least 3 days before placing furniture and 7 days before heavy use.

What is the best concrete finish for a patio?

Broom finish is the most popular and affordable option in Des Moines because it provides good traction when wet. Stamped concrete gives a decorative stone or brick look at a higher price. Des Moines Concrete Team can show you samples of each finish.

Will a concrete patio crack over time?

Some hairline cracking is normal in any concrete surface. Des Moines Concrete Team installs control joints in every Des Moines patio to direct cracking to planned lines where it is barely visible. Proper base preparation and curing minimize cracking significantly.

Do I need a permit for a patio in Des Moines?

Permits for patios in Des Moines depend on the size and location. Most ground-level patios under a certain square footage do not require one, but raised patios or those near property lines may. Des Moines Concrete Team checks permit requirements for you before starting work.
