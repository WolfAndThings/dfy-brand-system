The Real Cost of DIY

The DIY approach looks cheaper on paper, but when you add up the hidden costs — fuel, dump fees, rental fees, your time at your hourly rate, and the risk of injury — professional service is often within $50-$100 of DIY for most jobs.

When DIY Makes Sense

  • Small, lightweight items you can easily carry and fit in your vehicle
  • You have free time and enjoy the physical work
  • The disposal site is close and you know the rules and fees

When to Hire Tacoma Junk Crew

  • Heavy items (appliances, furniture, concrete, yard waste)
  • Items with disposal restrictions (Freon, e-waste, tires, paint)
  • Time-sensitive situations (moving day, tenant turnover, estate deadline)
  • Large volume that would require multiple trips
  • Physical limitations or safety concerns
