Frequently Asked Questions

How much does residential junk removal cost in Tacoma?

Residential junk removal in Tacoma typically costs between $150 and $500 for a standard pickup, depending on the volume and type of items. Tacoma Junk Crew provides free on-site estimates so you know the exact price before we start. Call 253-555-0112 for your quote.

How long does a residential junk removal job take in Tacoma?

Most residential junk removal jobs in Tacoma take between 30 minutes and 2 hours. Larger whole-home cleanouts may take a full day. Tacoma Junk Crew works efficiently to minimize disruption to your schedule.

Do I need to be home during junk removal?

You do not need to be home as long as the items are accessible and you have authorized the work in advance. Many Tacoma homeowners leave items in the driveway or garage for easy pickup. Just point us to the junk and we handle the rest.

What items can you haul away from my Tacoma home?

We haul furniture, appliances, electronics, yard waste, mattresses, boxes, and general household clutter. Tacoma Junk Crew recycles and donates usable items whenever possible. The only things we cannot take are hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, and asbestos.

Do you donate or recycle items you remove?

Tacoma Junk Crew partners with local Tacoma donation centers and recycling facilities to keep reusable items out of landfills. We sort materials on-site and donate furniture, clothing, and working appliances whenever possible.
