Frequently Asked Questions

How much does furniture removal cost in Tacoma?

Furniture removal in Tacoma typically costs $75 to $300 per item depending on size and weight. Couches and mattresses are on the lower end, while large sectionals and heavy dressers cost more. Tacoma Junk Crew offers multi-item discounts.

Will you pick up furniture from any room in my home?

Yes. Tacoma Junk Crew picks up furniture from any room in your Tacoma home including upstairs bedrooms, basements, and tight hallways. Our crew handles all the heavy lifting so you do not have to move anything yourself.

Do you donate furniture that is still in good condition?

Tacoma Junk Crew donates usable furniture to local Tacoma charities and thrift stores whenever possible. If your couch, table, or dresser is in decent shape, we make sure it goes to someone who can use it rather than a landfill.

Can you remove a mattress and box spring?

Yes. Mattress and box spring removal is one of our most common requests in Tacoma. Tacoma Junk Crew takes them to proper recycling or disposal facilities. Most mattress pickups cost between $75 and $125.

How fast can you pick up my old furniture?

Tacoma Junk Crew offers same-day furniture removal in Tacoma when you call before noon. Most furniture pickups take 15 to 45 minutes from arrival to cleanup. Call 253-555-0112 to book your time slot.
