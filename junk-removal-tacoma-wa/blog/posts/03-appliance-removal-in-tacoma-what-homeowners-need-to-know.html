Appliance Removal in Tacoma: What Homeowners Need to Know

Removing old appliances in Tacoma is harder than it looks. They are heavy, awkward, and some contain refrigerants that require special handling. Here is what Tacoma homeowners need to know about professional appliance removal.

Common Appliances We Remove

  • Refrigerators and freezers (require Freon recovery)
  • Washers and dryers
  • Dishwashers
  • Ovens, stoves, and ranges
  • Window and central AC units
  • Water heaters (gas and electric)
  • Microwaves and small appliances in bulk

Why You Should Not DIY Appliance Removal

  • 1. Weight — a standard refrigerator weighs 200-400 lbs. Moving one down stairs without proper equipment risks injury and property damage.
  • 2. Refrigerant laws — WA law requires certified technicians to recover Freon from refrigerators and AC units before disposal. Venting refrigerants is a federal violation.
  • 3. Disposal complexity — you cannot leave appliances at the curb in most Tacoma neighborhoods. Landfills charge extra for appliances and some require appointments.
  • 4. Reconnection risks — disconnecting gas appliances without shutting off the line is dangerous.

How Professional Appliance Removal Works

When you call Tacoma Junk Crew:

  • 1. Tell us what appliances need to go and where they are located
  • 2. We give you a price range over the phone (or send photos for exact pricing)
  • 3. Our crew arrives, disconnects the unit safely, and loads it
  • 4. We handle Freon recovery, recycling, and disposal
  • 5. The area is swept clean before we leave

What Happens to Old Appliances

We prioritize recycling and donation:

  • Working appliances go to local charities and resale shops
  • Metal components (steel, copper, aluminum) go to scrap recyclers
  • Refrigerants are recovered by certified technicians
  • Only non-recyclable materials go to landfill

Cost of Appliance Removal in Tacoma

Single appliance removal in Tacoma typically runs $75-$150. Multiple appliances on the same trip cost less per unit. Refrigerators and AC units with refrigerant may have a small surcharge for Freon recovery.

Here is a quick pricing breakdown:

  • Single small appliance (microwave, window AC): $50-$75
  • Single large appliance (washer, dryer, dishwasher): $75-$125
  • Refrigerator or freezer (includes Freon recovery): $100-$175
  • Water heater: $75-$125
  • Multiple appliances (3 or more on same trip): 15-25% discount per unit

When to Schedule Appliance Removal

The best time to schedule is the same day your new appliance gets delivered. Most delivery companies will only take the old unit if it is in the same room and already disconnected. If the delivery crew cannot take it, call us right away — we offer same-day pickup for most appliance jobs in Tacoma.

Planning a kitchen or laundry room renovation? Schedule your construction debris removal alongside the appliance pickup. Our crews can handle both in the same visit, which saves you money and hassle.

Appliance Removal FAQ

Can you remove a built-in appliance?

Yes. Our crews handle built-in ovens, microwaves, and dishwashers. We will disconnect and extract the unit. If you have a gas connection, make sure the gas line is shut off before we arrive.

Do I need to defrost my freezer first?

It helps, but it is not required. If the freezer is still running, unplug it the night before. If there is standing water, we will work around it.

Can you remove appliances from a second floor?

Absolutely. Stairs, narrow hallways, and tight corners are standard for our crews. We bring dollies and straps to move heavy units safely.
