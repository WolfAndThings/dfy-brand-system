Junk Removal vs Storage Unit: Which Saves You More?

Here is a pattern we see all the time in Tacoma: someone rents a storage unit "temporarily" while they figure out what to do with their stuff. Three years later, they have spent thousands of dollars storing items worth a fraction of that. If this sounds familiar, it is worth doing the math before renewing that storage contract.

The Real Cost of Storage in Tacoma

Storage unit prices in the Tacoma area vary by size and facility, but here are typical monthly rates:

  • 5x5 unit (closet-sized): $50-$80/month
  • 5x10 unit (walk-in closet): $80-$130/month
  • 10x10 unit (small bedroom): $120-$200/month
  • 10x20 unit (one-car garage): $180-$300/month
  • Climate-controlled: Add 20-30% to the above rates

Most people rent a 10x10 or 10x15 unit. At $150/month average, that is $1,800 per year. After two years, you have spent $3,600. After three years, $5,400. And the stuff inside is almost certainly depreciating in value the entire time.

The One-Time Cost of Junk Removal

Clearing out a 10x10 storage unit with full-service junk removal in Tacoma typically costs $300-$500. That is it. One payment, everything gone, no more monthly bill. Even a packed 10x20 unit rarely exceeds $600-$800 for complete cleanout.

When Storage Actually Makes Sense

  • Temporary moves: You are between homes and need 1-3 months of storage for furniture you will definitely use in your next place
  • Seasonal items: Boats, RVs, or seasonal equipment that you actively use part of the year
  • High-value items: Antiques, collectibles, or inherited items with genuine sentimental or monetary value that you cannot currently house
  • Business inventory: Active stock for a small business

When Junk Removal Is the Better Move

  • You have not visited the unit in 6+ months: If you are not using it, you do not need it
  • The contents are worth less than a year of rent: Used furniture, old electronics, and miscellaneous household items depreciate fast. A couch that was $1,200 new is worth $100-$200 used. Paying $1,800/year to store it makes no sense.
  • You are paying for "someday": Exercise equipment you will start using, project supplies you will get to, kids' toys they have outgrown — these are the classic storage unit fillers
  • The unit was inherited: Deceased relatives' storage units often contain items that family members feel guilty discarding but will never use

The Emotional Side

The hardest part of choosing junk removal over storage is not the money — it is the feeling of letting go. Many Tacoma homeowners tell us they felt relief, not regret, after clearing out a storage unit they had been paying for. The monthly expense disappears. The mental burden of "dealing with the storage unit" goes away. And the items that were sitting in a dark box get donated to someone who will actually use them.

How to Clear Out a Storage Unit in Tacoma

If you decide to go the junk removal route, here is the process:

  • 1. Walk through the unit and pull out anything you genuinely want to keep (be honest — if you have not touched it in a year, let it go)
  • 2. Call a junk removal company for a quote on the remaining contents
  • 3. The crew loads everything, donates usable items, recycles what they can, and disposes of the rest
  • 4. Cancel your storage contract and stop the monthly drain
