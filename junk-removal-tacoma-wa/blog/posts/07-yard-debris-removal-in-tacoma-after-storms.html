Yard Debris Removal in Tacoma After Storms

After storms hit Tacoma, yard debris piles up fast. Branches, leaves, and fallen limbs can overwhelm your regular trash service. Here is how to handle yard debris removal efficiently.

Common Yard Debris After Storms

  • Fallen tree branches and limbs
  • Leaves and brush piles
  • Broken fencing and lattice
  • Damaged patio furniture
  • Roof shingles blown into the yard
  • Standing water debris (plastic, lumber, misc)

What Your Regular Trash Service Will Not Take

Most Tacoma curbside trash services have limits on yard waste:

  • Branches must be under 4 feet and bundled
  • No stumps or root balls
  • Weight limits per bag or container
  • No mixed debris (cannot combine branches with construction waste)

When the pile exceeds those limits, you need a junk removal crew or a dump trailer.

DIY vs Professional Removal

| Factor | DIY | Professional |

|--------|-----|-------------|

| Cost | Dump fees + truck rental | Fixed quote |

| Time | Half day to full day | 1-2 hours |

| Labor | You load everything | Crew handles it |

| Disposal | You sort and haul | We handle it all |

For anything beyond a few bags of leaves, professional removal saves time and your back.

Storm Damage Tips for Tacoma Homeowners

  • 1. Document damage first — take photos for insurance claims before cleanup
  • 2. Check for downed power lines — do not touch any debris near lines, call WA power company
  • 3. Inspect your roof — look for missing shingles or damage from above
  • 4. Clear drains and gutters — prevent flooding from blocked water flow
  • 5. Call early — after major storms, crews book up fast
