Yard Debris Removal in Tacoma After Storms

After storms hit Tacoma, yard debris piles up fast. Branches, leaves, and fallen limbs can overwhelm your regular trash service. Here is how to handle yard debris removal efficiently.

Common Yard Debris After Storms

  • Fallen tree branches and limbs
  • Leaves and brush piles
  • Broken fencing and lattice
  • Damaged patio furniture
  • Roof shingles blown into the yard
  • Standing water debris (plastic, lumber, misc)

What Your Regular Trash Service Will Not Take

Most Tacoma curbside trash services have limits on yard waste:

  • Branches must be under 4 feet and bundled
  • No stumps or root balls
  • Weight limits per bag or container
  • No mixed debris (cannot combine branches with construction waste)

When the pile exceeds those limits, you need a junk removal crew or a dump trailer.

For anything beyond a few bags of leaves, professional removal saves time and your back.

Storm Damage Tips for Tacoma Homeowners

  • 1. Document damage first — take photos for insurance claims before cleanup
  • 2. Check for downed power lines — do not touch any debris near lines, call WA power company
  • 3. Inspect your roof — look for missing shingles or damage from above
  • 4. Clear drains and gutters — prevent flooding from blocked water flow
  • 5. Call early — after major storms, crews book up fast

Cost of Storm Debris Removal in Tacoma

Pricing depends on the volume and type of debris:

  • Small cleanup (a few branches, scattered debris): $150-$300
  • Medium cleanup (downed tree limbs, fencing, a full yard): $300-$600
  • Large cleanup (multiple trees, structural debris, full property): $600-$1,500+
  • Stump removal: typically quoted separately, $100-$400 per stump depending on size

We quote based on volume, not hours. You know the price before we start.

What to Expect During Cleanup

Here is the typical process after you call:

  • 1. Send photos or describe the damage — we can quote most storm jobs from photos alone
  • 2. We schedule within 24-48 hours — during heavy storm weeks, we prioritize by safety risk (downed trees blocking driveways or near power lines come first)
  • 3. Crew arrives with chainsaws and trailer — we cut, load, and haul everything in one visit when possible
  • 4. Green waste goes to composting facilities — branches and organic debris are recycled, not landfilled
  • 5. Final sweep — we rake and clean the area so your yard looks like the storm never happened

Tacoma Storm Season: When to Prepare

Tacoma's storm season runs roughly from October through March. The Puget Sound area sees windstorms, heavy rain, and occasional ice storms during these months. A few things to do before storm season hits:

  • Trim dead branches — anything dead or hanging over your roof should come down before wind does it for you
  • Secure outdoor furniture — bring lightweight items inside or anchor them
  • Clean gutters — clogged gutters cause water damage that looks like storm damage but is not covered by insurance
  • Know your insurance policy — most homeowner policies cover storm damage removal, but you need documentation (photos, receipts) to file a claim

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my homeowner insurance cover storm debris removal?

In most cases, yes — if the debris caused damage to your property or is blocking access. Document the damage with photos before cleanup begins, and keep your removal receipt. Your insurance company will need both for the claim.

Can you remove a tree that fell on my fence or shed?

Yes. We handle fallen trees on fences, sheds, decks, and other structures. If the tree is on or near power lines, we coordinate with the utility company before starting work. Safety first.

How quickly can you get here after a storm?

During normal weeks, same-day or next-day. After major storms that affect all of Tacoma, response times stretch to 2-5 days because demand spikes. Calling early gets you higher on the list.
