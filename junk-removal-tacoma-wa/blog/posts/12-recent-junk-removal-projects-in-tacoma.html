Recent Junk Removal Projects in Tacoma

Here is a look at recent Junk Removal projects completed by Tacoma Junk Crew in Tacoma and surrounding areas. These real jobs show the range of work we handle every week.

Garage Cleanout — Tacoma

A homeowner in central Tacoma had accumulated 15 years of storage in a two-car garage. Items included old furniture, boxes of paperwork, holiday decorations, broken exercise equipment, and dozens of bags of clothing. Our two-person crew completed the job in 3.5 hours. We donated 8 boxes of clothing and recycled all cardboard and metal.

Tenant Turnover — Near Downtown Tacoma

A property manager called with a two-bedroom unit that needed full cleanout before new tenants moved in. The previous tenant left a couch, mattress, kitchen table, bags of trash, and miscellaneous items in every room. Completed in 2 hours with broom-sweep finish. The unit was listed the next day.

Appliance Swap — Tacoma Suburbs

A couple upgrading their kitchen needed the old refrigerator, dishwasher, and stove removed the same day the new ones were delivered. We arrived 2 hours before the delivery window, removed all three appliances, and handled Freon recovery on the fridge. Total time on site: 45 minutes.

Estate Cleanout — Tacoma Area

A family settling their parents' estate needed the entire contents of a 3-bedroom home removed. We worked over two days, filling three full truckloads. Donated usable furniture to a local shelter, recycled metals and electronics, and left the home broom-clean for the real estate agent.

Storm Debris — After Recent Weather

After heavy storms hit Tacoma, we handled dozens of yard debris calls in a single week. Typical jobs included fallen branches, broken fencing, scattered shingles, and damaged patio furniture. Most storm debris jobs were completed within 1-2 hours.
