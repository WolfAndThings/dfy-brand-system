Full-Service vs Self-Load Junk Removal

There are two main models for getting junk out of your Tacoma home: full-service (a crew does everything) and self-load (a container is dropped off and you fill it). Both remove junk, but the experience and cost structure are very different. Here is what you need to know.

What Is Full-Service Junk Removal?

Full-service means a two-person crew arrives with a truck, enters your home, and physically removes everything you want gone. They carry items from any floor, load the truck, sweep up, and haul everything away. You do not lift a finger. Most pickups in Tacoma take 30-90 minutes.

What Is Self-Load Junk Removal?

Self-load services (like PODS, Bagster, or local container companies) drop a container in your driveway. You fill it on your own schedule over 1-7 days. When finished, you call for pickup. The container company hauls it to a disposal facility. Companies like Waste Management also offer curbside Bagster bags that hold about 3 cubic yards.

Pros and Cons of Full-Service

Pros

  • No physical labor — essential for seniors, people with injuries, or anyone who does not want to haul heavy items
  • Done in a single visit, often same-day
  • No container taking up driveway space
  • Crew handles stairs, tight spaces, and heavy items
  • Donation and recycling sorting included

Cons

  • Slightly higher cost for large volumes compared to self-load
  • You need to be present during the appointment
  • Not ideal if debris accumulates over multiple days (renovation projects)

Pros and Cons of Self-Load

Pros

  • Lower cost for large volumes since you provide the labor
  • Fill at your own pace — good for multi-day projects
  • No scheduling a specific appointment time

Cons

  • All loading is on you — heavy lifting, risk of injury
  • Container sits in your driveway for days
  • Weight limits and overage fees can surprise you
  • May need a city permit for street placement in Tacoma
  • No sorting — everything goes to the same place (usually landfill)

Which Is Right for You?

Choose full-service if you want a hands-off experience. Estate cleanouts, apartment cleanouts, hoarding situations, and any project where items are spread throughout a home are much easier with a crew. If your time has value and you would rather spend your Saturday doing something other than hauling trash, full-service pays for itself.

Choose self-load if you are doing a multi-day renovation and need a container on-site to toss materials into as you work. Contractors and DIY remodelers benefit from having a dumpster available throughout the project. If you are physically capable and budget-conscious, self-load saves $50-$150 on large loads.

Tacoma-Specific Advice

In Tacoma, full-service junk removal is available same-day from several local companies. Self-load containers typically require 24-48 hour advance scheduling for delivery. If you are in a Tacoma neighborhood with narrow streets or no driveway, full-service is often the only practical option since containers need flat, accessible placement. Apartments and condos in downtown Tacoma and the Stadium District almost always require full-service due to building access restrictions.
