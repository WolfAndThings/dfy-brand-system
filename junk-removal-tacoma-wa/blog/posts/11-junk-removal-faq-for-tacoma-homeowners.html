Junk Removal FAQ for Tacoma Homeowners

Here are the most common questions Tacoma homeowners ask about Junk Removal. If you do not see your question, call 253-555-0112 and we will answer it live.

How much does junk removal cost in Tacoma?

Pricing depends on volume. A single item like a couch or mattress runs $75-$150. A half truckload is $250-$400. A full truck is $400-$600. We give you an exact price before starting — no surprises. See our full pricing breakdown.

Do you offer same-day pickup?

Yes. If you call before noon on weekdays, we can usually arrive the same day. Weekend availability varies. Book same-day service by calling 253-555-0112.

What items do you take?

Almost everything: furniture, appliances, electronics, mattresses, yard debris, construction waste, hot tubs, sheds, and more. Items we cannot take include hazardous chemicals, medical waste, and full propane tanks. See our residential services for the full list.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. If items are accessible (driveway, garage, backyard) and you can share access instructions, we can complete the job while you are away. We send before-and-after photos.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Tacoma Junk Crew is fully licensed in WA and carries general liability insurance and workers compensation. We can provide a Certificate of Insurance on request for commercial clients.

Do you recycle or donate?

Absolutely. We sort every load. Usable furniture and appliances go to local Tacoma charities. Metal, cardboard, and electronics go to recyclers. Only non-recyclable items go to landfill.

How long does the job take?

Most jobs take 30-90 minutes. Estate cleanouts and large commercial jobs may take 4-8 hours. We give you a time estimate when you book.

What areas do you serve?

We cover Tacoma and all surrounding communities. Check our service areas page for the full list.

How do I get a quote?

Call 253-555-0112 or send us photos by text. We will give you a price range within minutes — no obligation, no visit required for most jobs.

Do you charge extra for stairs or difficult access?

Most jobs have no extra charge for stairs. If items are in a tight basement, a third-floor walkup, or require special handling (like a hot tub removal from a fenced backyard), we factor that into the upfront quote. You always know the price before we start — no surprise fees after the fact.

What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?

No cancellation fees. Life happens. If you need to reschedule, just call or text before the crew arrives. We ask for at least 2 hours notice so we can rebook the slot for another customer.

Can I watch while you work?

You are welcome to supervise, but it is not required. Many customers walk through the job at the start, point out what goes and what stays, then go about their day while we work. We send a text when we are done.

How is pricing determined?

We price by volume — how much space your items take up in our truck. A single couch takes about 1/8 of a truck. A full garage cleanout might fill the whole truck. Here is a general guide:

  • 1-2 items: $75-$200
  • 1/4 truck: $150-$250
  • 1/2 truck: $250-$400
  • 3/4 truck: $350-$500
  • Full truck: $400-$600

See our full pricing page for detailed breakdowns by job type.
