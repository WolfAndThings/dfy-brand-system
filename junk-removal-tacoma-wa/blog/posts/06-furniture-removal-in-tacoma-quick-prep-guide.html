Furniture Removal in Tacoma: Quick Prep Guide

Getting rid of old furniture in Tacoma? Professional furniture removal saves you the back pain and the trip to the dump. Here is a quick prep guide.

Items We Pick Up

  • Couches, sofas, and sectionals
  • Mattresses and box springs
  • Dining tables and chairs
  • Bedroom sets (bed frames, dressers, nightstands)
  • Desks and bookcases
  • Recliners and ottomans
  • Outdoor furniture (patio sets, umbrellas)

How to Prepare for Pickup

  • 1. Empty drawers and shelves — remove all personal items
  • 2. Clear a path — make sure the crew can get the furniture out without obstacles
  • 3. Remove bedding — strip sheets, pillows, and mattress covers
  • 4. Note the location — upstairs, basement, or garage affects crew time
  • 5. Disassemble if possible — taking legs off a table makes it easier to move through doorways

What Happens to Your Furniture

  • Good condition items — donated to local shelters and thrift stores in Tacoma
  • Damaged but usable — checked for potential refurbishment
  • Beyond repair — properly disposed of or recycled (wood, metal, foam separated)

Cost of Furniture Removal in Tacoma

  • Single item (couch, mattress): $75-$150
  • 2-3 items: $150-$300
  • Full room or house: quote based on volume

Why Not Just Leave It at the Curb?

Most Tacoma neighborhoods have bulk pickup restrictions. Items left at the curb can result in HOA fines or code violations. Tacoma Junk Crew handles everything — from your door to proper disposal.
