Landlord Turnover Cleanouts in Tacoma

Tenant moved out and left a mess? Landlord turnover cleanouts in Tacoma are one of our most common calls. Here is how to handle them fast and get the unit back on the market.

What a Turnover Cleanout Includes

  • Removing all abandoned furniture and personal property
  • Hauling out mattresses, trash bags, and loose debris
  • Clearing the garage, patio, and storage areas
  • Removing old appliances if you are upgrading
  • Sweeping and basic cleanup after removal

Legal Requirements in WA

Before throwing anything out, know the rules:

  • WA abandoned property law — you may need to store belongings for a set period and provide written notice before disposal
  • Document everything — photograph the unit before, during, and after cleanout
  • Keep records — save receipts from the removal company for deposit deduction claims
  • Hazardous materials — if the tenant left chemicals, paint, or other hazardous items, they require special disposal

How Fast Can We Turn It Around?

Tacoma Junk Crew can typically complete a turnover cleanout within 24-48 hours of booking:

  • Studio/1BR: 1-2 hours
  • 2-3BR: 2-4 hours
  • Full house with garage: 4-8 hours

We work around your contractor schedule — if painters, carpet cleaners, or maintenance crews need the space first, we coordinate.

Pricing for Landlords

We offer volume-based pricing and recurring service discounts for property managers with multiple units:

  • Light cleanout (few items): $150-$300
  • Moderate cleanout: $300-$600
  • Heavy or full-contents: $600-$1,500+

Build a Turnover System

Smart Tacoma landlords build junk removal into their turnover SOP:

  • 1. Final walkthrough with tenant (document condition)
  • 2. Issue abandoned property notice if needed
  • 3. Schedule Tacoma Junk Crew for removal
  • 4. Contractors handle repairs, painting, and carpet
  • 5. Unit photos and listing go live
