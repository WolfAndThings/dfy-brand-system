Commercial Junk Removal for Property Managers in Tacoma

Property managers and business owners in Tacoma need commercial junk removal that works around tenant schedules, lease timelines, and building regulations. Here is how it works.

Common Commercial Jobs in Tacoma

  • Tenant turnover cleanouts — clearing units between leases
  • Office furniture removal — desks, cubicles, chairs, and filing cabinets
  • Retail fixture removal — display cases, shelving, signage
  • Construction debris — drywall, lumber, flooring, and tile
  • Warehouse and storage cleanouts — pallets, equipment, and old inventory

Why Commercial Junk Removal Is Different

Commercial jobs have constraints that residential work does not:

  • Building access rules — loading docks, freight elevators, and restricted hours
  • Liability requirements — your building may require COI (Certificate of Insurance) from the removal company
  • Volume — commercial cleanouts often fill multiple truckloads
  • Timeline — turnover deadlines mean the job must be done by a specific date
  • Disposal compliance — some materials (electronics, fluorescent bulbs, chemicals) have regulated disposal requirements

How Tacoma Junk Crew Handles Commercial Work

  • 1. Site walkthrough — we visit the property to assess volume and access
  • 2. Written quote — you get a fixed price before work begins
  • 3. Scheduled service — we work around your building hours and tenant needs
  • 4. COI on request — we carry full liability coverage and can provide documentation
  • 5. Post-job cleanup — broom-swept and ready for the next tenant

Pricing for Commercial Jobs

Commercial pricing depends on volume, access, and materials. Typical ranges:

  • Single office cleanout: $300-$800
  • Full tenant turnover: $500-$2,000
  • Warehouse or large facility: custom quote

Property Manager Partnership Program

If you manage multiple properties in Tacoma, we offer ongoing service agreements that save time and money:

  • Priority scheduling — your calls go to the front of the line
  • Volume pricing — discounted rates for recurring work across multiple properties
  • Single point of contact — one crew lead who knows your buildings and access requirements
  • Monthly invoicing — no need to process individual payments for each job
  • COI on file — updated annually and available to your tenants and building owners immediately

Whether you manage 5 units or 500, we can build a service plan that fits your turnover schedule and budget.

E-Waste and Electronics Disposal

Commercial cleanouts frequently involve electronics that cannot go into standard waste:

  • Computers and monitors — must be recycled per WA e-waste regulations
  • Printers and copiers — contain toner cartridges that are classified as hazardous
  • Fluorescent lighting — tubes and CFLs contain mercury and require certified recycling
  • Data-bearing devices — hard drives and servers may need certified data destruction before disposal

Tacoma Junk Crew partners with certified e-waste recyclers in the Tacoma area to ensure your company stays compliant with WA disposal regulations.
