Our Junk Removal Guarantee — Tacoma Junk Crew

We do not hide behind fine print. When you hire Tacoma Junk Crew for junk removal in Tacoma, here is exactly what we guarantee — in plain language.

The Tacoma Junk Crew Guarantee

Upfront Pricing — No Surprises

We quote before we touch anything. The price we give you is the price you pay. If the job turns out smaller than expected, you pay less. If we underestimated, we honor the original quote. You will never get a bill higher than what was agreed upon.

On-Time Arrival

We give you a 2-hour arrival window and we hit it. If we are running late for any reason, we call before the window closes — not after. Respect for your time is not negotiable.

Complete Cleanup

When we leave, the area is cleaner than we found it. We do not just grab the big items and leave crumbs behind. We sweep, wipe down, and make sure the space is ready to use immediately.

Responsible Disposal

We sort every load. Recyclables go to recycling. Usable items are donated to local charities. Hazardous materials are routed to proper disposal facilities. We divert 40-60% of what we haul from landfills.

100% Satisfaction

If you are not completely satisfied with any aspect of our service, tell us before we leave and we will fix it on the spot. If you notice an issue after we have gone, call within 24 hours and we will come back at no charge to make it right.

What Is NOT Covered

  • Items added after the original quote without a revised estimate
  • Access changes that require additional equipment or labor (locked gates, elevator restrictions not disclosed)
  • Hazardous materials not identified during the quoting process (asbestos, lead paint, biohazards)

How to File a Concern

Call 253-555-0112 and ask for a manager. Describe the issue and we will resolve it within 24 hours. We log every concern and use them to improve our service. Your feedback makes us better.
