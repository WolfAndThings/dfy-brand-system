What Affects Your Appliance Removal Price

No two jobs are exactly alike. Here are the main factors that move your final cost up or down:

  • Appliance size and weight
  • Whether disconnection is needed (gas, water, electrical)
  • Floor level and stair access
  • Number of appliances in one trip
  • Freon-containing units (extra EPA handling)

A reputable company will explain exactly which of these factors apply to your specific situation and how they affect your quote. If a company cannot clearly explain their pricing, that is a red flag.

How to Save Money on Appliance Removal in Tacoma

  • Schedule multiple appliance removals in one visit for per-unit discounts
  • Ask if disconnection is included — some companies charge extra for gas or water line disconnect
  • Check with your local utility for appliance recycling rebate programs before paying for removal
  • Defrost freezers and drain water heaters before the crew arrives to speed up the job
