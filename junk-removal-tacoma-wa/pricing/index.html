How much does junk removal cost in Tacoma?+
Most junk removal jobs in Tacoma cost between $75 and $700. A single item pickup runs $75-$175, a quarter truck load is $175-$300, and a full truck load is $450-$700. Get an exact quote by calling 253-555-0112.
What items do junk removal companies not take?+
Most junk removal companies in Tacoma won't haul hazardous waste, chemicals, asbestos, paint, or medical waste. Tacoma Junk Crew handles almost everything else — furniture, appliances, electronics, yard waste, and construction debris.
Do I need to be home for junk removal?+
Not always. If your items are accessible from the curb, driveway, or garage, we can remove them without you being home. Just point us to the items during your quote call and we'll handle the rest.
