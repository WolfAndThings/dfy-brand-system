Junk Removal FAQ

Common questions about our Junk Removal services in Tacoma, WA.

How fast can you get here?
We offer same-day service for most jobs. Call in the morning and we can usually be there by afternoon. Emergency service is available for urgent situations.
Do you provide free estimates?
Yes — always. We quote by phone, text, or on-site. Send us a few photos and we can usually give you a price range within minutes.
What areas do you serve?
Tacoma and all surrounding communities. Check our service areas page for the full list.
How does pricing work?
We price by volume — the amount of space your items take up in our truck. You get an exact quote before we start, and the price never changes once approved.
Do I need to be home?
Not always. If items are accessible and you can share entry instructions, we can complete the job while you are away. We send photos when complete.
What items do you take?
Furniture, appliances, electronics, yard debris, construction waste, mattresses, hot tubs, and more. We will let you know about any restricted items on the call.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. We are fully licensed and carry liability insurance and workers comp for the protection of your property and our crew.

Still have questions?

Call us and we will walk you through everything.
