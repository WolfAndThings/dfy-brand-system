Emergency & Same-Day Junk Removal in Tacoma, WA

Need junk gone today? Tacoma Junk Crew provides emergency and same-day junk removal across Tacoma and surrounding areas. When the timeline is tight, we move fast — call now and we dispatch within hours.

When to Call for Emergency Junk Removal

  • Hoarding situations — health department deadlines, family interventions, or property access issues requiring immediate clearing
  • Eviction cleanouts — tenant has vacated and the unit needs to be cleared for the next lease or court deadline
  • Fire or flood debris — damaged materials, waterlogged furniture, and contaminated items that need to go before mold sets in
  • Code violation deadlines — city or HOA has issued a compliance notice with a hard deadline for property cleanup
  • Estate emergencies — property needs to be cleared for a closing, inspection, or probate deadline
  • Construction site overflows — debris pile has exceeded capacity and the crew cannot continue working

Response Time

For true emergencies, we aim to have a crew on-site within 2-4 hours during business hours. Same-day service is available for most jobs when you call before noon. After-hours and weekend emergency dispatch is available — response times may be slightly longer but we prioritize urgent situations.

What to Expect

  1. Call or text with details — tell us what needs to go, send photos if possible, and let us know your deadline. We assess urgency and give you a quote immediately.
  2. Crew dispatch — we confirm a window and dispatch the nearest available crew with the right truck for your job. You get a text when they are en route.
  3. Rapid loading and removal — our crew loads everything, sorts recyclables and donations where possible, and works until the space is clear.
  4. Final walkthrough and cleanup — before we leave, we sweep the area clean and walk through with you to confirm nothing was missed. You get a receipt on the spot.

After-Hours Availability

Our standard hours are 7 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week. For genuine emergencies — flood damage, fire debris, or situations with immediate health or safety concerns — we offer after-hours dispatch. Call the main line and follow the emergency prompt, or text us any time. We monitor messages around the clock.
